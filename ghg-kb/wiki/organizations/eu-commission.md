## Overview

The European Commission is the executive body of the European Union, responsible for proposing legislation, implementing EU law, and managing the EU budget. In the GHG emissions ecosystem, the Commission is the primary regulatory authority for both mandatory corporate sustainability reporting (CSRD/ESRS) and the EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS).

The Commission acts primarily through:
- **Directives** — require Member State transposition into national law (e.g., CSRD Directive 2022/2464, ETS Directive 2003/87/EC as amended)
- **Delegated Regulations** — directly applicable law adopted by the Commission under powers delegated by the Parliament and Council (e.g., ESRS delegated regulation 2023/2772)
- **Implementing Regulations** — technical implementing measures (e.g., MRR 2018/2066, AVR 2018/2067 for EU ETS)

## Role in GHG Ecosystem

**CSRD and ESRS:**
- The Commission proposed the CSRD directive, adopted by Parliament and Council in 2022
- Under Article 29b of the amended Accounting Directive, the Commission adopts ESRS via delegated acts based on EFRAG's technical recommendations
- The Commission adopted the first ESRS set as Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772 on 31 July 2023
- The Commission proposed the "Omnibus Package" in February 2025 to amend CSRD scope and delay certain deadlines (pending final adoption as of 2026-04-24)

**EU ETS:**
- The Commission administers the EU ETS in conjunction with Member States
- Sets the annual cap and manages the auctioning process
- Issues delegated and implementing regulations governing MRV (Monitoring, Reporting and Verification): MRR (2018/2066) and AVR (2018/2067)
- Operates the Union Registry — the central ledger of EU allowances

**CBAM:**
- The Commission administers the **Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism** under Regulation (EU) 2023/956 (as amended by Regulation (EU) 2025/2083) — the carbon-leakage instrument complementing EU ETS free-allocation phase-down (→ [[regulations/cbam|CBAM]])
- Operates the **CBAM Registry** (Article 14) — central electronic database for authorised CBAM declarants, certificate transactions, registered third-country operators/installations, and registered accredited verifiers
- Manages the **common central platform** for selling CBAM certificates (Article 20) from 2027-02-01; certificate price is the weekly average of EU ETS auction closing prices (Article 21)
- Adopts implementing acts setting Annex IV default values, EU ETS-aligned system boundaries, and Article 31 free-allocation adjustment rules
- Conducts oversight reviews of CBAM declarations (Article 19), risk-based controls (Article 15), de minimis monitoring (Article 25a), and circumvention investigations (Article 27)

**Relevant Directorates-General:**
- **DG FISMA** (Financial Stability, Financial Services and Capital Markets Union) — responsible for CSRD and ESRS policy
- **DG CLIMA** (Climate Action) — responsible for EU ETS, CBAM, and climate regulation
- **DG TAXUD** (Taxation and Customs Union) — supports CBAM through customs surveillance and the Article 25 customs-data exchange

**Sustainable Finance / EU Taxonomy:**
- The Commission proposed and the Parliament/Council adopted **Regulation (EU) 2020/852 (the EU Taxonomy Regulation)** establishing the classification framework for environmentally sustainable economic activities (→ [[regulations/eu-taxonomy|EU Taxonomy]])
- The Commission adopts the **technical screening criteria (TSCs)** via delegated act under Articles 10(3), 11(3), 12(2), 13(2), 14(2) and 15(2) — operationalised in the **Climate Delegated Act (Regulation (EU) 2021/2139)** for mitigation/adaptation and the **Environmental Delegated Act (Regulation (EU) 2023/2486)** for the four other objectives
- The Commission adopted the **Article 8 Delegated Act (Regulation (EU) 2021/2178)** specifying the content, presentation, and KPI methodology for Taxonomy disclosures by undertakings under non-financial reporting obligations
- The Commission chairs and operates the **Platform on Sustainable Finance** (Article 20) — the expert advisory body on TSC development and review

## Key Publications

- **Directive 2022/2464 (CSRD)** — the mandatory sustainability reporting directive
- **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772** — first set of ESRS standards (→ [[regulations/esrs-e1|ESRS E1]])
- **Directive 2003/87/EC as amended (ETS Directive)** — founding and governing legislation for EU ETS (→ [[regulations/eu-ets|EU ETS]])
- **Directive (EU) 2023/959** — 2023 "Fit for 55" ETS revision: Phase 4 cap acceleration (LRF stepped to 4.3%/4.4%; one-time rebasings −90 M in 2024 and −27 M in 2026), maritime transport inclusion from 2024, new ETS2 trading system for buildings/road transport/additional sectors (Chapter IVa, trading from 2027), CBAM-factor phase-out of free allocation 2026–2034, MSR amendments, Social Climate Fund linkage (→ [[sources/eu-directive-ets-revision-2023-959|Directive 2023/959 — Source]])
- **Directive (EU) 2023/958** — sister directive covering aviation-specific Phase 4 revisions (CORSIA, free-allocation phase-out for aircraft operators, sustainable aviation fuel allowances) — *not yet ingested*
- **Regulation (EU) 2023/955** — Social Climate Fund Regulation (parallel to Directive 2023/959) — *not yet ingested*
- **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/2066 (MRR)** — EU ETS monitoring and reporting rules
- **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/2067 (AVR)** — EU ETS verification and accreditation rules
- **Regulation (EU) 2023/956 (CBAM)** — establishes the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (→ [[regulations/cbam|CBAM]])
- **Regulation (EU) 2025/2083** — first major CBAM amendment (October 2025); adds the de minimis exemption, restructures certificate sale/surrender timing, and tightens anti-circumvention rules
- **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2830** — auction rules referenced for CBAM certificate price calculation
- **Regulation (EU) 2020/852 (EU Taxonomy Regulation)** — classification framework for environmentally sustainable economic activities; six environmental objectives, four-part alignment test, Article 8 disclosures (→ [[regulations/eu-taxonomy|EU Taxonomy]])
- **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2139 (Climate Delegated Act)** — TSCs for substantial contribution to climate change mitigation (Annex I, ~90 NACE-coded activities) and adaptation (Annex II, ~120+ activities including financial/insurance, education, health, arts) under the EU Taxonomy; sets the universal **<100 g CO2e/kWh life-cycle** threshold for power and heat generation; per-product GHG-intensity benchmarks for cement, iron and steel, aluminium, hydrogen, and chemicals derived from EU ETS product benchmarks (Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/447); zero-tailpipe-CO2 thresholds for road, rail, air, and water transport (with M1 transitional rules for fossil gas Sections 4.29–4.31 and nuclear Sections 4.26–4.28); five DNSH Appendices (A–E) referencing IPCC/EPBD/IED/REACH/Habitats; consolidated 08.01.2025 incorporating amendments 2022/1214 (M1 — nuclear/gas), 2023/2485 (M2), 2024/3215 (M3) (→ [[sources/eu-taxonomy-climate-delegated-act-2021-2139|Climate Delegated Act 2021/2139]])
- **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2486 (Environmental Delegated Act)** — TSCs for water/marine, circular economy, pollution, biodiversity under the EU Taxonomy
- **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2178 (Article 8 Delegated Act)** — KPI templates and methodology for Taxonomy disclosures by non-financial and financial undertakings
