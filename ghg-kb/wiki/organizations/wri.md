## Overview

The World Resources Institute (WRI) is a US-based environmental non-governmental organization and one of the two co-conveners of the [[organizations/ghg-protocol-initiative|GHG Protocol Initiative]] (alongside [[organizations/wbcsd|WBCSD]]). WRI provides the institutional home and secretariat functions for the GHG Protocol Initiative.

(→ [[sources/ghg-protocol-corporate-standard|GHG Protocol Corporate Standard]])

## Role in GHG Ecosystem

WRI co-develops and maintains the GHG Protocol standards suite. In the context of this knowledge base, WRI is relevant primarily as:
- Co-author of the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard, Scope 2 Guidance, and Scope 3 Standard
- Publisher of GHG Protocol calculation tools available at ghgprotocol.org
- Host of the Green Power Market Development Group (referenced in case studies for Scope 2 renewable procurement)
