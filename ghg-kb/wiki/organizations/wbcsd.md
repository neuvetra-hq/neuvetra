## Overview

The World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD) is a Geneva-based coalition of international companies focused on sustainable development. It is one of the two co-conveners of the [[organizations/ghg-protocol-initiative|GHG Protocol Initiative]] (alongside [[organizations/wri|WRI]]). At the time of the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard (Revised Edition), WBCSD comprised approximately 170 international companies.

(→ [[sources/ghg-protocol-corporate-standard|GHG Protocol Corporate Standard]])

## Role in GHG Ecosystem

WBCSD co-develops and maintains the GHG Protocol standards suite with WRI. WBCSD's business membership base ensures that the standards are practical for corporate users and reflect real-world implementation experience. Several WBCSD member companies (Shell, Holcim, Ford, Tata Steel) contributed case studies to the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard.

WBCSD also runs sector-specific sustainability initiatives (e.g., the Cement Sustainability Initiative) that have developed industry-specific GHG calculation tools in partnership with the GHG Protocol.
