## Key Takeaways

1. **Formally initiates the California APA rulemaking process** for Title 17, CCR §§96070–96077. This notice is the legal trigger for the public comment period and the February 26, 2026 board hearing.

2. **Confirms sections affected:** Proposed adoption of Title 17 CCR §§96070, 96071, 96072, 96073, 96074, 96075, 96076, and 96077.

3. **Confirms SB 261 injunction in formal rulemaking record.** Footnote 1 of this notice formally states: "On November 18, 2025, the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals issued an order temporarily enjoining enforcement of SB 261 until an appeal challenging SB 261 is resolved. In light of this order, CARB will not enforce SB 261 until the injunction is lifted. CARB will nevertheless continue this rulemaking to consider the Proposed Regulation for adoption."

4. **Economic impact classified as non-major.** Maximum estimated annual cost: $20,800,000 (FY 2026-27 through FY 2028-29). Total projected CARB fee revenue FY 2026–2031: $90,200,000. Annual costs fall below the $50M threshold that would trigger major regulation classification under H&S Code §57005.

5. **No comparable federal regulations exist.** CARB notes the SEC climate disclosure rules were finalized March 6, 2024, but voluntarily stayed and not advanced to implementation. This regulation is necessary to ensure California stakeholders receive climate information regardless of federal action.
