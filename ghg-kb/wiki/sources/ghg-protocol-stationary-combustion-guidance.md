## Key Takeaways

1. **Tiered approach for CO₂:** Tier 1 uses published default emission factors; Tier 2 uses facility-specific fuel carbon content from lab analysis; Tier 3 uses continuous emissions monitoring (CEMS). For most companies, Tier 1 with EPA Factors Hub values is sufficient.

2. **CH₄ and N₂O require technology context:** Unlike CO₂, methane and nitrous oxide factors depend on combustion temperature, equipment type, and maintenance. The guidance provides tables of CH₄ and N₂O factors by fuel type and combustion technology.

3. **Biomass CO₂ is excluded from Scope 1 total:** Biogenic CO₂ from wood, biomass, and biofuels is reported separately as a memo item. CH₄ and N₂O from biomass combustion are still included in Scope 1.

4. **Covers all fixed combustion sources:** boilers, furnaces, turbines, emergency generators, flares, compressor stations, startup fuel — including infrequently used equipment.

5. **Does not cover mobile combustion** (vehicles) or Scope 2 electricity purchases.
