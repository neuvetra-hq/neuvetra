## Key Takeaways

1. **August 10, 2026 is the official, legally binding Scope 1/2 first-year reporting deadline under SB 253.** This aligns with the MRR verification deadline to streamline reporting obligations. The statute required a 2026 report but did not specify a date; this regulation sets it. (§96076)

2. **"Revenue" is officially defined as gross receipts under RTC §25120(f)(2).** This definition is gross — it does not deduct cost of goods sold or operating costs. This matters especially for high-volume/low-margin businesses (retailers, distributors) whose GAAP revenue might look smaller than their gross receipts. (§96072(a)(13))

3. **"Doing business in California" anchors to RTC §23101(a) plus only criteria (b)(1) or (b)(2).** The property-holding [§23101(b)(3)] and payroll [§23101(b)(4)] prongs are explicitly excluded to avoid catching companies whose only California presence is a small office or remote employees. (§96072(a)(7)–(8))

4. **Revenue threshold uses the lesser of the two prior fiscal years.** An entity must fall below threshold for two consecutive years to exit the program — preventing one-off asset sales cycling companies in and out of compliance obligations. (§96072(a)(5), (11))

5. **The Initial Regulation is fee + first-year deadline only.** All other requirements — reporting content, format, data verification, future years' deadlines, enforcement provisions — are deferred to a subsequent rulemaking. First-year reports may be based on best-available data; entities not collecting data at the time of the December 2024 Enforcement Notice are not expected to submit for 2026. (§96076; ISOR §F)

## Codified Exemptions (§96071)

Entities NOT subject to this regulation even if they meet revenue thresholds:
- Non-profit or charitable organizations (IRC tax-exempt)
- Business entities regulated by the California Department of Insurance, or in the insurance business in any other state
- Federal, state, and local government entities, and companies majority-owned (>50%) by government entities
- Business entities whose **only** California activity is wholesale electricity transactions
- Business entities whose **only** California presence is teleworking employees

## Fee Formula

Annual fee calculated separately for SB 253 (§38532) and SB 261 (§38533):

```
RRy = RRy-1 × [1 + ANNy]         # Required Revenue, CPI-adjusted (starting FY 2027)
TRRy = (RRy × (1 + 0.10)) + D    # Total Required Revenue with 10% contingency + debt
F38532 = (TRRy × P38532) / N38532  # SB 253 flat fee per entity
F38533 = (TRRy × P38533) / N38533  # SB 261 flat fee per entity
```

Where P = allocated share of staff/budget, N = number of covered entities, D = GGRF loan repayment. Fee notices issued by **September 10** each year; payment due within **60 days**.
