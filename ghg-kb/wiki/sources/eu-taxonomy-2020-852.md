## Key Takeaways

1. **The Taxonomy is a classification system, not an emissions calculation regime.** It establishes the criteria for determining whether an *economic activity* (not a company) qualifies as environmentally sustainable, in order to channel capital flows toward sustainable investment and prevent greenwashing.
2. **Six environmental objectives (Article 9):** climate change mitigation; climate change adaptation; sustainable use and protection of water and marine resources; transition to a circular economy; pollution prevention and control; protection and restoration of biodiversity and ecosystems. Each has its own substantial-contribution criteria (Articles 10–15) and its own Commission Delegated Act establishing the technical screening criteria (TSCs).
3. **Four-part test for alignment (Article 3):** an economic activity qualifies as environmentally sustainable only if it (a) substantially contributes to one or more of the six objectives, (b) does not significantly harm any of the others (the "DNSH" principle, Article 17), (c) is carried out in compliance with minimum safeguards (Article 18 — OECD MNE Guidelines, UN Guiding Principles, ILO fundamental conventions, International Bill of Human Rights), and (d) complies with technical screening criteria adopted by the Commission via delegated act.
4. **Article 8 disclosure obligation:** undertakings subject to NFRD/CSRD non-financial reporting must disclose the proportion of their **turnover, capital expenditure (CapEx), and operating expenditure (OpEx)** that is associated with Taxonomy-aligned economic activities. Specified by the Article 8 Delegated Act (Regulation (EU) 2021/2178), and reported under the CSRD environmental information block per ESRS 1 Appendix D.
5. **Delegation framework:** the Regulation itself contains no TSCs — they are delegated to the Commission. The Climate Delegated Act (Regulation (EU) 2021/2139) covers mitigation + adaptation TSCs and was adopted by the 31 December 2020 deadline (Article 10(6), 11(6)). The Environmental Delegated Act covers TSCs for the four remaining objectives. Article 19 sets the requirements that all TSCs must satisfy (technological neutrality, life-cycle, scientific evidence, quantitative thresholds where possible, exclusion of solid fossil fuel power generation, etc.). Article 20 establishes the Platform on Sustainable Finance to advise the Commission on TSC development.
