## Key Takeaways

1. **Board voted on February 26, 2026** to adopt the California Corporate Greenhouse Gas Reporting and Climate-Related Financial Risk Disclosure Initial Regulation implementing SB 253 and SB 261 (as amended by SB 219).

2. **August 10, 2026 confirmed as the first-year SB 253 reporting deadline** for Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions.

3. **Scope 3 reporting begins in 2027**, with the schedule set by CARB regulation (per SB 219's delegation of authority).

4. **120+ climate-related financial risk reports** had already been voluntarily submitted ahead of the January 1, 2026 SB 261 deadline, per CARB's count at the time of the board vote.

5. **CARB will apply enforcement discretion for good-faith first-year submissions.** Scope 1 & 2 only for year one; first-year reports may be based on best-available data.

## Key Quote

CARB Chair Lauren Sanchez: *"By establishing clear and consistent disclosure requirements, California is ensuring that the state's investors and consumers have access to reliable information."*
