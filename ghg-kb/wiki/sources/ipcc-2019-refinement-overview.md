## Key Takeaways

1. **Refinement, not revision.** The 2019 Refinement does not replace the 2006 Guidelines — it updates them where gaps were identified or where post-2006 science matured sufficiently. Inventory compilers must still consult the 2006 Guidelines for unchanged sections. Each volume contains a *Mapping Tables* annex showing per-section status (Update / New Guidance / No Refinement / Removed). Where a section is labelled Update or New Guidance, the 2019 Refinement contains all material needed; the 2006 Guidelines need not be consulted for that section.

2. **Tier framework unchanged: Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3.** Tier 1 = global default factors applied to broad activity data; Tier 2 = country/region-specific factors with finer-resolution activity data; Tier 3 = facility-specific or model-based methods with site-level data. Higher tiers are more accurate when data are available. The "good practice" definition is unchanged: procedures producing inventories that are systematically neither over- nor under-estimates and that minimize uncertainty. (→ [[methodologies/afolu-ipcc-tiers|AFOLU IPCC Tier Framework]])

3. **Five volumes follow the 2006 structure.**
    - **Vol. 1** — General Guidance and Reporting (national arrangements, data collection strategy, facility-level data integration, uncertainty, key category analysis, atmospheric verification, model reporting)
    - **Vol. 2** — Energy (only fugitive emissions updated; stationary and mobile combustion unchanged)
    - **Vol. 3** — IPPU (new categories: hydrogen production, rare earth metals, alumina via Bayer-Sinter / Nepheline, MEMS, electronic-circuit waterproofing; updates to nitric acid, fluorochemicals, iron & steel, aluminium, electronics, RAC equipment)
    - **Vol. 4** — AFOLU (Tier 3 modeling refined; new flooded-lands methodology; updated Tier 1 biomass and soil-carbon factors; livestock and manure factor updates; soil N2O direct/indirect EFs disaggregated by climate region; updated rice cultivation factors; HWP methods updated for stock-change/production/simple-decay/atmospheric-flow approaches)
    - **Vol. 5** — Waste (FOD model updated; MSW open burning oxidation factor updated; gasification/pyrolysis CH4 and N2O EFs added; septic and centralized wastewater EFs updated; new N2O EFs for domestic and industrial WWTP; new appendix on non-biogenic CO2 from wastewater)

4. **AFOLU livestock and manure: significant Tier 1 updates.** Methane conversion rate (Ym) for cattle and buffalo is now diet- and productivity-dependent. Methane conversion factor (MCF) for animal-waste-management systems is now keyed to climate region (not annual mean temperature) with a simple monthly-temperature model. High- vs low-productivity differential EFs are added for major animal categories. Treatment of nitrogen transfers between livestock categories and to agricultural soils is improved.

5. **Soil carbon: Tier 1 stock-change factors updated.** Many updated factors reflect a *smaller* anthropogenic impact on soil carbon than the 2006 defaults, based on a global dataset analysis. Reference C stocks updated for soil-type × climate-region combinations. New Tier 2/3 methods for biochar amendment effects on cropland and grassland mineral-soil C. New steady-state Tier 2 alternative for Cropland Remaining Cropland.

6. **Soil N2O: direct + indirect EFs disaggregated by climate region.** A key Tier 1 development; previously a single global EF1 = 0.01 kg N2O-N/kg N applied was used.

7. **New flooded-land methods.** CO2 and non-CO2 emissions from Land Converted to Flooded Lands and from Flooded Land Remaining Flooded Land are moved from appendix-level (2006) to main guidance (2019), reflecting matured science. Methods cover total emissions via Managed Land Proxy plus an optional method for indicative anthropogenic component.

8. **IPPU adds new sources and gases.** Production of hydrogen, rare earths, alumina (Bayer-Sinter, Nepheline), and waterproofing of circuit boards are now methodologically covered. Additional HFCs, PFCs, halogenated ethers (e.g., PFPMIE — used as electronic heat-transfer fluid), and other gases identified in IPCC Fourth and Fifth Assessment Reports are included where anthropogenic sources exist and are not in Annexes A–E of the Montreal Protocol.

9. **Energy: only fugitive emissions updated.** Stationary combustion, mobile combustion, and other Energy sources have *no* methodological updates in the 2019 Refinement. Fugitive updates cover: coal (CO2 from underground/surface mines, year-specific abandoned-mine factors 2017–2050); oil & gas (unconventional production EFs, abandoned wells, definitions annex); fuel transformation (charcoal, biochar, coke including flaring, coal-to-liquids, gas-to-liquids — appendices for biomass-to-liquids/gas and wood pellets).

10. **Waste FOD model and EFs updated.** First-Order-Decay model with updated waste-generation rates and composition by country/region; default values and uncertainty for carbon, nitrogen, and DOC of domestic and industrial sludge; updated DOCf values; new MCF values for actively-aerated landfill at different management levels; new gasification and pyrolysis CH4/N2O EFs; updated MSW-open-burning oxidation factor; new and updated N2O EFs for domestic and industrial WWTPs.
