## Key Takeaways

1. **First adopted set of European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) under CSRD.** Bundles 12 standards as Annex I: 2 cross-cutting (ESRS 1, ESRS 2) + 5 environmental topical (E1–E5) + 4 social topical (S1–S4) + 1 governance topical (G1). All are sector-agnostic; sector-specific standards remain to be adopted in subsequent delegated acts.

2. **ESRS 1 (General Requirements) codifies the architecture and double-materiality framework that governs all other standards.** Defines impact materiality (severity × likelihood, anchored in UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights + OECD Guidelines), financial materiality (effect on cash flow / financial position / cost of capital), and the requirement to assess both. Sets reporting period = financial reporting period; short-term = financial reporting period, medium-term ≤ 5 years, long-term > 5 years.

3. **ESRS 2 (General Disclosures) is the cross-cutting backbone.** Every topical standard plugs into ESRS 2 via four reporting areas: GOV (governance), SBM (strategy/business model), IRO (impact/risk/opportunity management), MT (metrics/targets). Includes 4 Minimum Disclosure Requirements (MDR-P policies, MDR-A actions, MDR-M metrics, MDR-T targets) that structure how every topical disclosure is presented.

4. **Climate change has special status — the only topic where non-materiality requires a detailed forward-looking justification.** ESRS 1 paragraph 32: if an undertaking concludes climate is non-material, it must provide a "detailed explanation of the conclusions of its materiality assessment with regard to climate change, including a forward-looking analysis of the conditions that could lead the undertaking to conclude that climate change is material in the future." For all other topics, a brief explanation suffices. This is the regulation's strongest signal that climate is the de-facto baseline topic.

5. **Phase-in provisions are extensive and tied to a 750-employee threshold.** Appendix C of ESRS 1 lists phased-in disclosures: undertakings ≤750 employees may omit Scope 3 + Total GHG (E1-6) for year 1, and may omit ALL of E4, S1, S2, S3, S4 disclosures for the first 1–2 years. E1-9, E2-6, E3-5, E4-6, E5-6 (anticipated financial effects) may be qualitative-only for the first 3 years. SBM-1 sector breakdown is deferred until a future Commission Delegated Act on ESRS sectoral classification.

6. **Value chain reporting is mandatory but proxy estimation is permitted.** Reporting boundary extends beyond financial consolidation to include material upstream/downstream value chain impacts. Where direct collection is impractical (especially for SME suppliers), undertakings shall use sector-average data and other proxies — explicitly endorsed for Scope 3 calculation. A 3-year transitional provision allows exclusion of value-chain metric information not reasonably available, subject to disclosure of efforts.

7. **The 12 standards covering all material ESG topics:**

| Standard | Topic | Disclosure Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| ESRS 1 | General Requirements (architecture, materiality, value chain, time horizons) | None — sets framework for all others |
| ESRS 2 | General Disclosures (cross-cutting GOV/SBM/IRO/MT/MDR) | BP-1, BP-2, GOV-1 through GOV-5, SBM-1 through SBM-3, IRO-1, IRO-2, plus MDR-P, MDR-A, MDR-M, MDR-T |
| ESRS E1 | Climate Change | E1-1 through E1-9 (9 DRs) |
| ESRS E2 | Pollution | E2-1 through E2-6 (6 DRs) |
| ESRS E3 | Water and Marine Resources | E3-1 through E3-5 (5 DRs) |
| ESRS E4 | Biodiversity and Ecosystems | E4-1 through E4-6 (6 DRs) |
| ESRS E5 | Resource Use and Circular Economy | E5-1 through E5-6 (6 DRs) |
| ESRS S1 | Own Workforce | S1-1 through S1-17 (17 DRs) |
| ESRS S2 | Workers in the Value Chain | S2-1 through S2-5 (5 DRs) |
| ESRS S3 | Affected Communities | S3-1 through S3-5 (5 DRs) |
| ESRS S4 | Consumers and End-Users | S4-1 through S4-5 (5 DRs) |
