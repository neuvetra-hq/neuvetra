## Key Takeaways

1. **Template is voluntary for 2026.** Released to streamline reporting, especially for first-time reporters. Explicitly does not modify or supersede the statute, does not impose requirements, and does not have force of law. CARB will address subsequent cycles through rulemaking.

2. **Template structure — 8 sections:** Organization Information; Third-Party Verification; Inventory Boundary; Scope 1 and Scope 2 Disclosure (by emission source, with optional by-GHG breakdown); Methodology; De Minimis/Minor Sources; California MRR Fields (if applicable); Emission Reductions (if applicable). Optional future fields include base year emissions for intraorganizational comparison.

3. **Minimum decision-useful fields:** At least one intensity metric (e.g., MT CO2e per $M revenue); NAICS 2-digit sector classification; disclosure of GWP values (source and year); emission factor source; calculation approach (e.g., activity data × emission factor); MRR facility ID numbers to cross-reference with the CARB MRR database.

4. **Open policy question — organizational boundary approach:** CARB was evaluating whether to mandate one approach (equity share, financial control, or operational control) across all reporters for comparability, or retain flexibility. Unresolved as of the October 2025 comment period; may be addressed in subsequent rulemaking.

5. **Emission reductions field:** Optional field for reductions from direct contracts for renewable electricity and renewable gas. CARB sought public input on whether to add other reduction types or targets.
