## Key Takeaways

1. **Dual reporting is required** — companies with any operations in markets providing contractual instruments (EU, US, Australia, Japan, India, most Latin American countries) must report scope 2 using both the location-based method AND the market-based method, labelled separately.

2. **Market-based method** uses GHG emission rates from qualifying contractual instruments (RECs, Guarantees of Origin, PPAs, supplier-specific rates) that meet all 8 Scope 2 Quality Criteria. Instruments failing the Criteria are ineligible; the residual mix or location-based factor is used as substitute.

3. **8 Scope 2 Quality Criteria** — minimum standards for instrument integrity: (1) conveys direct GHG emission rate, (2) unique claim (not double-counted), (3) tracked and retired, (4) close vintage to reporting period, (5) same market as consumption, (6) utility-specific factor based on delivered electricity, (7) direct contracts must be third-party verified, (8) residual mix available or absence disclosed.

4. **Residual mix** — the emission factor for unclaimed/untracked electricity in a market. Required to prevent double counting. EU Association of Issuing Bodies (AIB) publishes European residual mixes. If unavailable, companies use location-based grid average and disclose the absence.

5. **Scope 2 covers all purchased energy inputs** — electricity, steam, heat, and cooling (see Appendix A of the guidance for steam/heat/cooling accounting).
