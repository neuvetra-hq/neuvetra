## Key Takeaways

1. **Scope 3 schedule delegated entirely to CARB.** The original SB 253 statute required Scope 3 disclosure "no later than 180 days after" Scope 1 and 2 disclosures. SB 219 removed this fixed relationship, replacing it with "a schedule specified by the state board." This is the legal basis for the 2027 Scope 3 start date in the adopted CARB regulation.

2. **Parent-level consolidation explicitly authorized.** A parent company may consolidate SB 253 and SB 261 reporting for qualifying subsidiaries in a single report. Subsidiaries of qualifying parent companies need not file separately. This was added to both §38532 and §38533.

3. **Annual fee requirement removed from both statutes.** The original SB 253 and SB 261 statutory texts required entities to pay an annual fee "upon filing the disclosure." SB 219 removed this language from both statutes — not to eliminate fees, but to move fee authority into CARB's rulemaking process. The Initial Regulation (Title 17 CCR §§96070–96077) is the resulting fee mechanism.

4. **CARB contracting authority changed from mandatory to discretionary.** Both statutes originally required CARB to contract with an emissions reporting organization and a climate reporting organization respectively. SB 219 made this discretionary: CARB "may" contract with such organizations "to perform tasks it deems appropriate." This gives CARB flexibility to perform functions directly.

5. **CARB's regulation adoption deadline extended to July 1, 2025** (from the original January 1, 2025). The adopted regulation was ultimately finalized February 26, 2026, after the Board extended the timeline further through the rulemaking process.

## Statutory Changes — SB 253 (H&S Code §38532)

| Provision | Before SB 219 | After SB 219 |
|---|---|---|
| Regulation adoption deadline | January 1, 2025 | July 1, 2025 |
| Scope 3 disclosure timing | No later than 180 days after Scope 1/2 | On a schedule specified by the state board |
| Parent consolidation | Not specified | Explicitly authorized |
| Fee upon filing | Required annually | Removed (moved to regulation) |
| Contracting with reporting org | Shall | May |
| Subsidiary filing | Required | Not required if parent consolidates |

## Statutory Changes — SB 261 (H&S Code §38533)

| Provision | Before SB 219 | After SB 219 |
|---|---|---|
| Contracting with reporting org | Shall | May, for tasks "it deems appropriate" |
| Fee upon filing | Required annually | Removed (moved to regulation) |
| Subsidiary consolidation | Already included | Clarified |

## Definitions Preserved (Unchanged)

- **Reporting entities (SB 253):** U.S. businesses with annual revenues exceeding $1 billion doing business in California
- **Covered entities (SB 261):** U.S. businesses with annual revenues exceeding $500 million doing business in California
- GHG Protocol alignment requirement for Scope 1, 2, and 3 calculations preserved
