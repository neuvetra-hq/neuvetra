## Key Takeaways

1. **SB 261 is temporarily enjoined by the Ninth Circuit.** On November 18, 2025, the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals issued an order temporarily enjoining enforcement of SB 261 pending resolution of an appeal challenging the statute. CARB explicitly states it will not enforce SB 261 until the injunction is lifted. CARB nevertheless continued the rulemaking and adopted the regulation on February 26, 2026 — the fee regulation applies to both SB 253 and SB 261. (ISOR fn. 2; §F)

2. **Program cost: ~$20.8M/year (FY 2026-27 through FY 2028-29), dropping to ~$13.9M/year thereafter.** Total projected CARB revenue from fees: $90.2M over FY 2026–2031. Initial program setup (FY 2024-25 and 2025-26) was funded by a loan from the Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund (GGRF); fees will repay this debt. CARB is hiring up to 42 permanent positions across 4 new sections. (§II; §VIII)

3. **Revenue threshold uses the lesser of two prior fiscal years** — not just the most recent year. This prevents entities from cycling in and out of compliance due to one-off asset sales or other anomalous revenue spikes. The two-year average approach was adopted specifically to address this concern. (§96072(a)(5), (11); ISOR §B, §96072 rationale)

4. **"Doing business in California" excludes property and payroll prongs of RTC §23101.** CARB explicitly removed criteria (b)(3) and (b)(4) — which would catch companies with as little as $73,502 in California property or payroll — to avoid unintentionally sweeping in companies with only marginal California presence (e.g., a few teleworking employees). Only criteria (b)(1) (organized/domiciled in CA) or (b)(2) (California sales > $735,019 in 2024) trigger coverage. (ISOR §B, §96072(a)(7)–(8) rationale)

5. **August 10, 2026 chosen to align with the MRR verification deadline.** The proposed date matches CARB's existing Mandatory Reporting Regulation verification deadline with the goal of streamlining compliance timing for entities that report under both programs. Future years' deadlines will be set in a subsequent rulemaking. Stakeholder feedback requested at least 6 months after fiscal year end; the February 1 cutoff rule ensures this. (ISOR §F, §96076 rationale)

## Fee Structure Rationale

Three alternatives were considered and rejected:

| Alternative | Description | Reason Rejected |
|---|---|---|
| No fee | Fund program from existing CARB funds | Violates explicit statutory mandate; unsustainable; diverts funds from other programs |
| Monthly payments | Same annual total, collected monthly | Creates additional administrative burden for entities and CARB |
| Progressive (emissions-weighted) | Higher emitters pay more | Requires verified emissions data not yet available; could disincentivize full disclosure |

**Flat fee chosen** because: (a) no baseline emissions data exists yet, (b) uniform compliance obligations across all entities regardless of size, (c) easier to administer, (d) provides higher cost predictability.
