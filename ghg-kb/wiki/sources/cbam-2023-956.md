## Key Takeaways

1. **CBAM is the EU's carbon-leakage instrument complementary to EU ETS.** It applies an EU ETS-equivalent carbon price to imports of cement, electricity, fertilisers, iron and steel, aluminium, hydrogen, and selected precursor goods (Annex I) on entry into the EU customs territory. CBAM phases in as EU ETS free allocation to the same sectors phases out (Article 31).

2. **Two-phase rollout:** Transitional period 2023-10-01 to 2025-12-31 — quarterly CBAM reports only, no certificates surrendered (Articles 32-35). Definitive period from 2026-01-01 — full obligations apply: authorised CBAM declarant status mandatory, annual CBAM declaration with verified embedded emissions, surrender of CBAM certificates corresponding to embedded emissions less any third-country carbon price paid and less the EU ETS free-allocation adjustment.

3. **First definitive declaration:** By 30 September 2027, for calendar year 2026 (Article 6(1) as amended by Regulation (EU) 2025/2083). From 1 February 2027 Member States sell CBAM certificates on a common central platform; certificate price = weekly average of EU ETS auction closing prices (quarterly average for 2026 emissions).

4. **De minimis exemption (Article 2a, added by 2025/2083):** Importers below a 50-tonne net-mass annual threshold (Annex VII) are exempt — designed to capture ~99% of embedded emissions while excluding small/occasional importers. Does NOT apply to electricity or hydrogen. Anti-circumvention rules treat artificial splitting as a serious infringement.

5. **Penalties:** Authorised declarants who fail to surrender face the EU ETS excess-emissions penalty (€100/tonne, inflation-indexed per Article 16(3)-(4) of Directive 2003/87/EC) per missing certificate, and surrender obligation persists. Non-authorised importers introducing covered goods face 3-5x that penalty per missing certificate. Exceeding the de minimis threshold without authorisation triggers paragraph 2 penalty over the entire calendar year's emissions.

6. **Embedded emissions methodology (Annex IV):** Direct emissions from production processes plus indirect emissions from electricity consumed (indirect not counted for goods listed in Annex II — iron/steel, aluminium, hydrogen). Calculated either from actual operator data verified by an accredited verifier (Article 8, Annex VI) or by reference to Commission default values (country-specific or EU average for electricity; country-average + mark-up for other goods, or top-10 emission-intensity benchmark when reliable data missing). System boundaries shall be aligned with EU ETS (Article 7(7)).

7. **Verifier accreditation under Regulation (EC) 765/2008** — same framework as the EU ETS AVR (Regulation (EU) 2018/2067); EU ETS-accredited verifiers' qualifications shall be taken into account for CBAM accreditation (Article 18(2)).

8. **Geographic exclusions (Annex III pt 1):** Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland; Büsingen, Heligoland, Livigno, Ceuta, Melilla. Conditional electricity exemption available for third countries with market-coupled, EU-aligned electricity systems committed to climate neutrality by 2050 (Article 2(7)).
