### 2. Inclusion threshold and covered entities (§95812, §95811)
Compliance obligation is triggered at **25,000 MT CO2e** of MRR-verified covered emissions per year. Covered entity types:
- First deliverers of electricity (in-state generators, electricity importers)
- Operators of facilities with annual covered emissions ≥ 25,000 MT CO2e in: cement, glass, hydrogen, iron and steel, lime, nitric acid, petroleum refining, pulp and paper, lead production, lime manufacture, mineral processing, oil and gas extraction, others
- Suppliers of natural gas (LDCs)
- Suppliers of RBOB, distillate fuel oil, LPG, and natural gas to end users
- Operators of CO2 suppliers

### 3. Compliance periods and true-up timing (§95840, Subarticle 6)
Operates on **three-year compliance periods** with annual interim surrenders:
- Compliance period 1: 2013–2014 (2-year transitional)
- Compliance period 2: 2015–2017
- Compliance period 3: 2018–2020
- Compliance period 4: 2021–2023
- Compliance period 5: 2024–2026
- Compliance period 6: 2027–2029
- Compliance period 7: 2030–2032

**Annual interim surrender:** ~30% of preceding-year covered emissions, due **November 1**.
**Triennial true-up:** Full surrender for 100% of compliance period covered emissions, due **November 1 of the year following period end**.

### 4. Compliance instruments (§§95820, 95821)
Three instrument types:
- **California GHG Allowances (CCAs)** — 1 metric ton CO2e each; vintage-dated; sold via quarterly auctions and partly free-allocated to industrial covered entities
- **ARB Offset Credits and Registry Offset Credits** — issued under CARB-approved Compliance Offset Protocols; subject to quantitative usage limit (LO)
- **Price Ceiling Units** — sold at the price ceiling (Subarticle 10); proceeds used to fund equivalent or greater additional GHG reductions

### 5. Quantitative offset usage limits — AB 398 reductions (§95854)
The fraction of compliance obligation that may be met with offsets is restricted, and was further restricted by AB 398 (2017):

| Compliance period | LO (offset usage limit) |
|---|---|
| Periods 1, 2, 3 (2013–2020) | 8% |
| Period 4 (2021–2023) | 4% |
| Period 5 (2024–2026) | **4% for data years 2024–2025; 6% for data year 2026** |
| Period 6+ (2027 onward) | 6% |

In addition (AB 398): **at least half of offsets used for compliance must come from projects with direct environmental benefits in California**.

### 6. Price ceiling and price floor mechanism (§§95911, 95913, 95915)
Three price-control instruments:
- **Auction Reserve Price (price floor)** — bids below this price are not accepted at quarterly auctions; rises annually
- **Allowance Price Containment Reserve (APCR)** — two reserve tiers; allowances released for sale at fixed price tiers if the auction settlement price approaches them
- **Price Ceiling** — hard maximum; the Executive Officer issues Price Ceiling Units at this price if demanded; proceeds fund additional GHG reductions on at least a ton-for-ton basis

### 7. Linkage with external systems (Subarticle 12)
The regulation provides legal infrastructure for linking California's program to external GHG ETSs. Currently linked to **Québec's cap-and-trade system** under the Western Climate Initiative (WCI). Linked allowances are interchangeable for compliance.

### 8. Offset protocols and project lifecycle (Subarticle 13)
ARB approves Compliance Offset Protocols (current set includes U.S. Forest Projects, Urban Forest Projects, Livestock, Ozone Depleting Substances, Mine Methane Capture, Rice Cultivation). Project lifecycle: listing → reporting → third-party verification → issuance of Registry Offset Credits → conversion to ARB Offset Credits → invalidation rules (§95985) within 8 years for forestry / 3 years for non-forestry.

**Forestry reversal rules (§95983):** Forest Buffer Account holds a portion of issued credits as insurance against reversals (intentional or unintentional carbon loss).

### 9. CITSS — central compliance infrastructure (Subarticle 5)
All entities holding compliance instruments must register in the **California Integrated Emissions Tracking System** (CITSS). Account types: General, Compliance, Holding, Limited Use Holding, Annual Allocation Holding. Each entity must designate Primary and Alternate Account Representatives and disclose Corporate Associations to prevent collusion at auction.

### 10. Auction structure and limits (§§95910–95914)
**Quarterly auctions** held in February, May, August, November via CITSS. Format: single-round, sealed-bid, uniform price.
- **Auction Purchase Limit:** caps the share of allowances any single entity (or affiliated group) may purchase
- **Holding Limit:** caps allowances any entity may hold at any time
- Auction results published; winning bidders charged the **Auction Settlement Price** (uniform clearing price)
