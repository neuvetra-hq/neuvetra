> **Pre-rulemaking notice:** This document presents CARB staff proposals as of 2025-08-21. The fee regulation was scheduled for CARB Board adoption at a public hearing on 2025-12-11/12. Proposals marked below may have been modified or finalized since this workshop. The adopted fee regulation should be ingested to supersede these proposals.

## Key Takeaways

1. **SB 219 is a third California climate disclosure law.** It amends both SB 253 and SB 261, clarifying CARB's authority to set the Scope 3 disclosure schedule (beginning 2027) and adding a parent-level consolidation option under SB 253.

2. **SB 253 Scope 1/2 deadline proposed as June 30, 2026.** CARB staff proposed this specific date (to be codified in the fee regulation), replacing the statutory "CARB-determined date." As of the workshop this was still a proposal; it may have been finalized by the December 2025 Board vote.

3. **Proposed exemptions not in the bill texts.** CARB staff proposed exempting: non-profit organizations; companies whose only California business is the presence of teleworking employees; entities whose only California activity consists of wholesale electricity transactions in interstate commerce (including CAISO). Government entities are already structurally excluded by statute.

4. **Fee structure is flat per entity.** Proposed annual fees: ~$1,403 for SB 261 entities (>$500M revenue); additional ~$3,106 for SB 253 entities (>$1B revenue). Companies subject to SB 253 pay both fees (~$4,509 total). Program ongoing cost: $13.9M/year; one-time setup: $20.7M (funded by Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund FY 23/24–24/25).

5. **SB 261 CARB docket: links to company reports.** CARB opened a public docket on 2025-12-01 for covered entities to submit links to their SB 261 climate risk reports. Docket closes 2026-07-01. Reports themselves are hosted on company websites; the docket is a central registry of links only.
