## Key Takeaways

1. **20 MW thermal input** is the key threshold for combustion installations — below this, facilities are not covered by EU ETS (though they may still be covered by CSRD/ESRS)
2. **Annual MRV cycle:** monitoring plan → verified annual emissions report submitted by **March 31** → surrender of EU allowances (EUAs) by **September 30**; failure to surrender triggers **€100/tonne** penalty (inflation-indexed), which does NOT discharge the obligation
3. **Phase 4 cap accelerating rapidly:** Linear reduction factor stepped up to 4.3%/year (2024–2027) and 4.4% from 2028; 2030 target is 62% reduction vs. 2005; 2024 cap was 1,386,051,745 allowances
4. **Aviation and maritime now covered** (maritime from 2024); ETS2 for buildings and road transport fuel distributors separate but linked (monitoring from 2025, trading from 2027)
5. **Operational control boundary applies:** The permit-holding operator of the installation bears compliance responsibility, not the financial owner — operational control approach is mandatory for EU ETS
