## Key Takeaways

1. **One chapter per category** — dedicated calculation guidance, worked examples, and data source recommendations for all 15 scope 3 categories, providing the operational detail not in the Scope 3 Standard itself.

2. **Four data quality tiers** — from most to least accurate: (1) supplier-specific activity data (primary data from value chain partners), (2) hybrid data (primary activity + secondary emission factors), (3) average industry data (sector averages), (4) spend-based data (EEIO models applied to procurement spend). Companies should target the highest tier for their largest emission categories.

3. **Category 1 (Purchased goods and services)** is typically the largest scope 3 category for most companies. Main calculation approaches: spend-based using EEIO emission factors, average material/product emission factors, or supplier-specific lifecycle data.

4. **Category 11 (Use of sold products)** is typically largest for energy-consuming product manufacturers (electronics, appliances, vehicles). Requires assumptions on product lifetimes and use profiles; results represent expected future emissions from products sold in the reporting year.

5. **Appendix D** provides formula summary tables for all 15 categories — practical reference for calculation implementation.
