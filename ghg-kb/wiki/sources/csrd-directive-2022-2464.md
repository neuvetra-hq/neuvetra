## Key Takeaways

1. **Double materiality** is the defining reporting principle — companies assess both impacts on people/planet (inside-out) and financial risks/opportunities from sustainability issues (outside-in)
2. **Scope expansion:** CSRD replaces the NFRD and covers all large EU undertakings (2 of 3: >250 employees, >€50M turnover, >€25M balance sheet), listed SMEs, and third-country companies with >€150M EU turnover
3. **ESRS mandate:** CSRD delegates all technical disclosure requirements to ESRS, adopted by the Commission via delegated act. First ESRS set adopted as Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772
4. **Phased rollout:** Large listed (FY2024) → all large (FY2025) → listed SMEs (FY2026) → third-country (FY2028)
5. **Assurance required:** All sustainability reports must be accompanied by a limited assurance opinion from an accredited provider
