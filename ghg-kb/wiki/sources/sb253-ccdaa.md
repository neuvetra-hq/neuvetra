## Key Takeaways

1. **Applies to any company doing business in California with >$1B revenue** — not just California-based companies. This is the single broadest applicability threshold in U.S. corporate GHG disclosure law.

2. **GHG Protocol is legally mandated** — the statute explicitly names the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard and Scope 3 Standard as the required accounting framework. Our wiki's GHG Protocol coverage directly serves SB 253 compliance.

3. **Scope 3 is required, not optional** — unlike the base GHG Protocol Corporate Standard which makes Scope 3 optional, SB 253 requires all three scopes. First Scope 3 disclosure is due 2027 (within 180 days of Scope 1/2 disclosure).

4. **Penalty cliff for Scope 3 during 2027–2030:** Only nonfiling is penalizable for Scope 3 in this period. Inaccurate-but-good-faith Scope 3 disclosures are explicitly protected from penalties. This creates a "file something reasonable" standard for early years.

5. **CARB implementing regulations were due January 1, 2025** — CARB is responsible for the specific reporting dates, platform, assurance provider qualifications, and fee structure. The first Scope 1/2 reporting date in 2026 is set by CARB, not fixed in statute.
