## Key Takeaways

1. **Four calculation methods, ranked by accuracy:** Material Balance (best) → Simplified Material Balance (recommended for most companies) → Screening (default factors) → Purchased Gas Method. Choose based on available data.

2. **Simplified Material Balance is the practical default:** Uses only refrigerant purchase records and disposal recovery records — data that should be available from service contractors. Formula: purchases − recovered at disposal = emissions.

3. **CFCs and HCFCs are excluded from CO₂e totals:** Ozone-depleting refrigerants are reported as separate memo items only, without GWP conversion. This matters for facilities still operating legacy equipment.

4. **GWP source:** EPA directs users to the GHG Emission Factors Hub for current GWP values. The wiki uses IPCC AR6 GWP100 values stored in the external database.

5. **Default screening leak rates by equipment type:** provided in Table 1 of the document (1–3% of charge per year depending on equipment category). These are conservative defaults with high uncertainty.
