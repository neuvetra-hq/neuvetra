## Key Takeaways

1. **Nine disclosure requirements (E1-1 through E1-9):** E1-6 (Gross Scopes 1, 2, 3) is the core quantitative GHG metrics disclosure; all values in metric tonnes CO₂e
2. **E1-1 (Transition plan) is mandatory regardless of materiality assessment** — the only E1 disclosure that cannot be waived even if climate is assessed as immaterial
3. **EU ETS linkage:** E1-6 requires disclosure of the percentage of Scope 1 emissions covered by EU ETS allowances — the critical quantitative bridge between the two EU GHG regimes
4. **GHG Protocol is the referenced methodology:** E1-6 calculations use the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard (Scope 1/3) and GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance (location-based and market-based Scope 2)
5. **Phase-in for Scope 3:** Year 1 opt-out available for the full 15-category Scope 3 breakdown; mandatory from Year 2 for all large companies
