# GHG Knowledge Base — Index
Last updated: 2026-04-25 | Total pages: 120

## Concepts (19)
- [[concepts/scope-1|Scope 1 — Direct GHG Emissions]] — Direct emissions from owned/controlled sources; mandatory to report
- [[concepts/scope-2|Scope 2 — Electricity Indirect GHG Emissions]] — Indirect emissions from purchased electricity/heat/steam/cooling; dual location-based and market-based reporting required
- [[concepts/scope-3|Scope 3 — Other Indirect GHG Emissions]] — Value chain indirect emissions; optional under GHG Protocol, mandatory under CSRD/SB 253; 15 standardized categories; ESRS Chapter 5 sector-average proxy permitted
- [[concepts/scope-3-categories|Scope 3 Categories]] — The 15 value chain emission categories (upstream 1–8, downstream 9–15) with minimum boundaries and calculation methods
- [[concepts/organizational-boundary|Organizational Boundary]] — Defines which entities are inside the company's inventory; set via equity share, financial control, or operational control
- [[concepts/operational-boundary|Operational Boundary]] — Defines which emission sources within the organizational boundary are Scope 1, 2, or 3
- [[concepts/base-year|Base Year]] — Fixed historical reference year for tracking emissions over time; recalculated on structural changes
- [[concepts/ghg-accounting-principles|GHG Accounting and Reporting Principles]] — Seven quality criteria: Relevance, Completeness, Consistency, Transparency, Accuracy (corporate); plus Conservativeness (project + land sector) and Permanence (land sector + removals, added by GHG Protocol Land Sector and Removals Standard)
- [[concepts/additionality|Additionality]] — Criterion that GHG reductions from a project only count if the project would not have occurred without climate motivation; central to offset integrity
- [[concepts/double-materiality|Double Materiality]] — EU-specific assessment principle requiring disclosure of both impact on people/planet (inside-out) and financial risks/opportunities (outside-in); severity = scale × scope × irremediability per UN Guiding Principles + OECD Guidelines; climate has special non-materiality forward-looking-justification rule
- [[concepts/co2-removals|CO2 Removals]] — Anthropogenic transfer of CO2 from atmosphere into long-term storage; distinct from emission avoidance; governed by GHG Protocol Land Sector and Removals Standard
- [[concepts/land-use-change-emissions|Land Use Change Emissions]] — GHG emissions and removals from changes in land use (deforestation, afforestation, peatland conversion); a major Cat 1 and Cat 15 factor for agriculture and finance sectors
- [[concepts/financed-emissions|Financed Emissions]] — GHG emissions attributable to a financial institution's loans and investments (Scope 3 Cat 15); calculated via PCAF attribution formula; typically 95–99% of a bank's total inventory
- [[concepts/esrs-phase-in|ESRS Phase-in and Transitional Provisions]] — 750-employee size-conditional reliefs, year-1 and 3-year qualitative-only reliefs, value-chain transitional provision, comparative-information relief; centralized reference for all CSRD/ESRS disclosure-level phase-in rules
- [[concepts/six-environmental-objectives|Six Environmental Objectives]] — EU Taxonomy Article 9 catalogue: climate change mitigation, climate change adaptation, sustainable use and protection of water and marine resources, transition to a circular economy, pollution prevention and control, protection and restoration of biodiversity and ecosystems
- [[concepts/taxonomy-eligibility|Taxonomy Eligibility]] — EU Taxonomy first-pass scope question: activity is on a Delegated Act catalogue with TSCs defined; necessary precursor to alignment; Article 8 eligibility KPIs reported as proportion of turnover/CapEx/OpEx
- [[concepts/taxonomy-alignment|Taxonomy Alignment]] — Operative status under Article 3 of EU Taxonomy: activity is eligible AND substantially contributes to one of six objectives AND passes DNSH for the other five AND meets minimum safeguards AND complies with activity-specific TSCs (e.g., 100 gCO2e/kWh for power generation)
- [[concepts/dnsh-do-no-significant-harm|Do No Significant Harm (DNSH)]] — Article 17 second leg of Taxonomy alignment: activity must not significantly harm any of the other five environmental objectives; life-cycle perspective; activity-specific DNSH criteria in Climate and Environmental Delegated Acts; layered with SFDR Article 2(17) DNSH
- [[concepts/minimum-safeguards|Minimum Safeguards]] — Article 18 third leg of Taxonomy alignment: undertaking-level procedures aligned with OECD MNE Guidelines, UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, ILO fundamental conventions, International Bill of Human Rights; covers human rights, bribery/corruption, taxation, fair competition

## Regulations (23)
- [[regulations/ab32|California Global Warming Solutions Act of 2006 (AB 32)]] — Foundational enabling statute; directs CARB to cap GHG emissions at 1990 levels by 2020; authorizes MRR, Cap-and-Trade, and all major CARB GHG programs
- [[regulations/carb-cap-and-trade|California Cap-and-Trade / Cap-and-Invest Regulation]] — Market-based GHG compliance mechanism (Title 17 CCR §§95801–96022); declining annual caps; quarterly auctions via CITSS; 25,000 MT CO2e threshold links to MRR; 2026 amendments in rulemaking
- [[regulations/sb253-ccdaa|California SB 253 — CCDAA]] — Requires >$1B revenue companies doing business in CA to report Scope 1/2 (August 10, 2026 — official) and Scope 3 (2027 per CARB schedule) per GHG Protocol; up to $500K/year penalty
- [[regulations/sb261|California SB 261 — Climate-Related Financial Risk Disclosure]] — Requires >$500M revenue companies doing business in CA to publish biennial TCFD-aligned climate risk reports; first due 2026-01-01 (enforcement suspended by Ninth Circuit injunction Nov 2025); up to $50K/year penalty
- [[regulations/sb219|California SB 219 — Climate Disclosure Amendments]] — Amends SB 253 and SB 261: clarifies CARB sets Scope 3 schedule; adds parent-level consolidation option
- [[regulations/carb-mrr|California Mandatory Reporting Regulation (CARB MRR)]] — Facility-level mandatory GHG reporting for California facilities and fuel suppliers; 10,000 MT CO2e threshold triggers reporting; 25,000 MT triggers verification and cap-and-trade compliance
- [[regulations/lcfs|California Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS)]] — Title 17 CCR §§95480–95503; market-based fuel-pool carbon-intensity standard administered by CARB under AB 32; declining annual CI benchmarks (gasoline 76.60 → 9.91 gCO2e/MJ from 2025 H2 to 2045) generate tradable credits/deficits in MTCO2e via LRT-CBTS; 2025 amendment OAL-approved 2025-06-27, effective 2025-07-01; up to $1,000 per uncleared deficit; Automatic Acceleration Mechanism active May 15, 2027
- [[regulations/cbam|EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)]] — Regulation (EU) 2023/956 (consolidated to 2025-10-20 incl. Reg (EU) 2025/2083); transitional 2023-10-01 to 2025-12-31 (quarterly reports only), definitive period from 2026-01-01 with first annual declaration and certificate surrender 2027-09-30 for 2026 emissions; covers EU imports of cement, electricity, fertilisers, iron/steel, aluminium, hydrogen; 50-tonne de minimis (excl. electricity/hydrogen); EU ETS-equivalent €100/tonne penalty per missing certificate, 3–5× for unauthorised importers
- [[regulations/eu-taxonomy|EU Taxonomy Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2020/852)]] — EU classification framework for environmentally sustainable economic activities; six environmental objectives, four-part Article 3 alignment test (substantial contribution + DNSH + minimum safeguards + TSCs), Article 8 KPI disclosures (turnover/CapEx/OpEx); operative TSCs for mitigation + adaptation now fully detailed via Climate Delegated Act 2021/2139 (Annex I 100 gCO2e/kWh power threshold, EU ETS-benchmark-derived cement/steel/aluminium thresholds, zero-tailpipe transport, fossil gas + nuclear transitional rules)
- [[regulations/csrd|EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)]] — Mandatory EU sustainability reporting for large companies (>250 employees / >€50M turnover / >€25M balance sheet) and listed SMEs; double materiality assessment required; ESRS are the technical standards adopted via Commission Delegated Regulation 2023/2772; phased rollout FY2024–FY2028
- [[regulations/esrs-1|ESRS 1 — General Requirements]] — Cross-cutting standard codifying ESRS architecture, double-materiality methodology, value-chain rules, time horizons, and transitional provisions; no Disclosure Requirements but governs how every other ESRS is applied
- [[regulations/esrs-2|ESRS 2 — General Disclosures]] — Cross-cutting standard with always-mandatory disclosures across BP/GOV/SBM/IRO + four Minimum Disclosure Requirements (MDR-P, MDR-A, MDR-M, MDR-T) that structure every topical policy/action/metric/target
- [[regulations/esrs-e1|ESRS E1 — Climate Change]] — GHG-specific EU reporting standard under CSRD; 9 disclosure requirements including Scope 1/2/3 metrics (E1-6), transition plan (E1-1, mandatory regardless of materiality), and EU ETS coverage percentage; GHG Protocol is the referenced methodology
- [[regulations/esrs-e2|ESRS E2 — Pollution]] — EU pollution disclosure standard under CSRD; 6 DRs covering air/water/soil pollutants, microplastics, and substances of (very high) concern; tied to E-PRTR and REACH frameworks
- [[regulations/esrs-e3|ESRS E3 — Water and Marine Resources]] — EU water disclosure standard under CSRD; 5 DRs with focus on water consumption in areas of high water stress; conventionally uses WRI Aqueduct or equivalent
- [[regulations/esrs-e4|ESRS E4 — Biodiversity and Ecosystems]] — EU biodiversity standard under CSRD; 6 DRs including a transition plan (E4-1) modeled on E1-1; addresses the five IPBES direct drivers; tied to EU Biodiversity Strategy 2030 and Kunming-Montréal GBF; longest size-conditional relief (2-year ≤750-employee)
- [[regulations/esrs-e5|ESRS E5 — Resource Use and Circular Economy]] — EU circular economy standard under CSRD; 6 DRs covering resource inflows, outflows including waste, secondary content; tied to EU Circular Economy Action Plan and Critical Raw Materials Act
- [[regulations/esrs-s1|ESRS S1 — Own Workforce]] — EU social standard under CSRD; 17 DRs (most of any ESRS) covering working conditions, equal treatment, work-related rights; ≤750-employee 1-year omission applies
- [[regulations/esrs-s2|ESRS S2 — Workers in the Value Chain]] — EU social standard under CSRD; 5 DRs on supply-chain worker rights and human-rights due diligence; ≤750-employee 2-year omission applies
- [[regulations/esrs-s3|ESRS S3 — Affected Communities]] — EU social standard under CSRD; 5 DRs on impacts on indigenous peoples, local communities, FPIC; ≤750-employee 2-year omission applies
- [[regulations/esrs-s4|ESRS S4 — Consumers and End-Users]] — EU social standard under CSRD; 5 DRs on consumer information, safety, social inclusion; ≤750-employee 2-year omission applies
- [[regulations/esrs-g1|ESRS G1 — Business Conduct]] — EU governance standard under CSRD; 6 DRs covering corporate culture, supplier payment practices, anti-corruption/bribery, lobbying; no size-conditional first-year relief
- [[regulations/eu-ets|EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS)]] — World's first international cap-and-trade system; **substantially revised by Directive (EU) 2023/959 (Fit for 55)**: Phase 4 LRF stepped to 4.3%/4.4% with one-time cap rebasings (−90 M 2024, −27 M 2026), 62% reduction by 2030 vs 2005; **maritime transport included from 2024** (40/70/100% phase-in; CH4/N2O from 2026); **new ETS2 (Chapter IVa) for buildings/road transport/additional sectors** with fuel suppliers as regulated entities (permits 2025, trading 2027, first surrender 2028); CBAM-factor phase-out of free allocation 2026–2034; €100/tonne penalty

## Methodologies (15)
- [[methodologies/double-materiality-assessment|Double Materiality Assessment (DMA) — Process and Value-Chain Extension]] — EFRAG IG 1 + IG 2 four-step assessment (context → identify → assess → report); severity-likelihood thresholds; financial-control vs operational-control boundary; tiered value-chain data approach; Seren Group worked example
- [[methodologies/scope-1-stationary-combustion|Scope 1 — Stationary Combustion]] — Calculates Scope 1 from fuel burned in fixed equipment (boilers, generators, furnaces); Emissions = Fuel (MMBtu) × EF; required by CARB MRR and SB 253
- [[methodologies/scope-1-mobile-combustion|Scope 1 — Mobile Combustion]] — Calculates Scope 1 from fuel burned in vehicles and mobile equipment under operational control (fleet, off-road, marine, rail); two methods (fuel-based recommended, distance-based fallback); v1 fuel-based spec executable pending `combustion-mobile` factor extraction from EPA Hub 2025 + DEFRA 2024
- [[methodologies/scope-1-fugitive-refrigerants|Scope 1 — Fugitive Refrigerant Emissions]] — Tracks HFC/PFC leaks from refrigeration and HVAC; four methods (Simplified Material Balance recommended); uses IPCC AR6 GWP100 values
- [[methodologies/scope-3-cat1-spend-based|Scope 3 Cat 1 — Spend-Based Method (EEIO)]] — Tier 4 USEEIO-based method; Emissions = Spend (USD2022) × NAICS factor; uses EPA Supply Chain GHG EF v1.3.0
- [[methodologies/scope-3-cat6-business-travel|Scope 3 Cat 6 — Business Travel]] — Distance-based method for flights, rail, taxi, car, ferry; Emissions = Distance × DEFRA factor; includes RF treatment for aviation
- [[methodologies/equity-share-approach|Equity Share Approach]] — Consolidates emissions proportional to ownership stake; one of three organizational boundary methods
- [[methodologies/financial-control-approach|Financial Control Approach]] — Consolidates 100% of emissions from financially controlled operations; one of three organizational boundary methods
- [[methodologies/operational-control-approach|Operational Control Approach]] — Consolidates 100% of emissions from operated facilities; required by EU ETS; one of three organizational boundary methods
- [[methodologies/project-specific-baseline|Project-Specific Baseline Procedure]] — Estimates baseline emissions for a mitigation project by identifying a scenario-specific counterfactual through barrier and net-benefits analysis
- [[methodologies/performance-standard-baseline|Performance Standard Baseline Procedure]] — Estimates baseline emissions using a benchmark emission rate derived from all comparable technologies or practices in the sector; reusable across multiple similar projects
- [[methodologies/scope-2-location-based|Scope 2 Location-Based Method]] — Calculates scope 2 using regional grid average emission factors; universally applicable; required by most mandatory reporting schemes
- [[methodologies/scope-2-market-based|Scope 2 Market-Based Method]] — Calculates scope 2 using emission rates from qualifying contractual instruments (RECs, GOs, PPAs); requires 8 Scope 2 Quality Criteria
- [[methodologies/scope-3-cat15-financed-emissions|Scope 3 Cat 15 — Financed Emissions (PCAF Method)]] — PCAF attribution formula (outstanding ÷ EVIC × borrower emissions); 10 asset classes; 1–5 data quality scoring; mandatory for financial institutions under CSRD and SB 253
- [[methodologies/afolu-ipcc-tiers|AFOLU IPCC Tier 1 / 2 / 3 Framework]] — IPCC tiered methodology for agriculture, forestry and land use GHG quantification; Tier 1 = default IPCC factors, Tier 2 = country-specific, Tier 3 = modelled; underpins CARB MRR and GHG Protocol land sector guidance

## Sectors (12)
- [[sectors/technology|Technology & Software]] — Asset-light (SaaS/software) or asset-heavy (hardware/data centers); Scope 2 electricity dominant; Cat 11 use-of-sold-products dominates hardware inventories
- [[sectors/restaurants-food-service|Restaurants & Food Service]] — Natural gas and refrigerants dominate Scope 1; Cat 1 purchased food (especially meat/dairy) typically 70–90% of total inventory; most operators below mandatory reporting thresholds
- [[sectors/retail-consumer-goods|Retail & Consumer Goods]] — Scope 3-dominated; Cat 1 purchased merchandise 60–90% of total inventory; grocery refrigerant leaks are the most-missed Scope 1 source
- [[sectors/financial-services|Financial Services]] — Cat 15 financed emissions 95–99% of total inventory; operational footprint small; insurance companies exempt from SB 253/261 under California law
- [[sectors/manufacturing|Manufacturing & Industrial]] — Scope 1-dominated; combustion + process emissions; CARB MRR and Cap-and-Trade compliance at facility level; process gases require source-specific methods
- [[sectors/real-estate-construction|Real Estate & Construction]] — Building energy (Scope 2) and refrigerant leakage dominate; Cat 13 tenant energy and Cat 1 embodied carbon are the defining Scope 3 sources; tenant-landlord boundary is the key accounting question
- [[sectors/transportation-logistics|Transportation & Logistics]] — Fleet fuel combustion is Scope 1 dominant for asset-heavy operators; Cat 4 contracted transport dominates for asset-light 3PLs; EU ETS covers aviation and maritime
- [[sectors/healthcare-pharmaceuticals|Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals]] — Anesthetic gases (high-GWP N2O, desflurane) are the most commonly missed Scope 1 source; Cat 1 supply chain and Cat 11 MDI inhaler propellants dominate Scope 3 for large operators
- [[sectors/agriculture-food-production|Agriculture & Food Production]] — Non-CO2 gases dominate (enteric fermentation CH4, soil N2O); IPCC Tier 1/2 methods required; Cat 1 agricultural inputs link farm emissions to downstream food companies
- [[sectors/energy-utilities|Energy & Utilities]] — Scope 1 combustion and fugitive CH4 are massive; Cat 11 sold energy products can dwarf all other categories; CARB MRR and Cap-and-Trade mandatory for California generators and fuel suppliers
- [[sectors/hospitality-hotels|Hospitality & Hotels]] — High energy intensity per guest-night; refrigerant leakage systematically underreported; Cat 1 food purchasing dominates Scope 3 for full-service properties
- [[sectors/professional-services|Professional Services]] — Near-zero Scope 1; Cat 6 business travel typically 80–90% of total inventory for consulting/advisory firms; Cat 7 employee commuting is the secondary source

## Organizations (7)
- [[organizations/ghg-protocol-initiative|GHG Protocol Initiative]] — WRI + WBCSD partnership; primary standard-setter for corporate GHG accounting globally
- [[organizations/wri|World Resources Institute (WRI)]] — US-based NGO; co-convener of the GHG Protocol Initiative
- [[organizations/wbcsd|World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD)]] — Geneva-based industry coalition; co-convener of the GHG Protocol Initiative
- [[organizations/carb|California Air Resources Board (CARB)]] — California state regulator; administers MRR, Cap-and-Trade, and SB 253 implementation
- [[organizations/efrag|European Financial Reporting Advisory Group (EFRAG)]] — EU independent body; develops ESRS standards under CSRD mandate; provides technical advice to the European Commission on sustainability reporting
- [[organizations/eu-commission|European Commission]] — EU executive body; proposes and adopts GHG legislation (CSRD, EU ETS); issues ESRS as delegated regulations; administers EU ETS cap and Union Registry
- [[organizations/pcaf|Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials (PCAF)]] — industry-led standard-setter; publishes three-part Global GHG Accounting Standard for financial institutions; 670+ signatories; authoritative source for Cat 15 (Scope 3) methodology
