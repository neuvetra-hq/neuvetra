## Overview

ESRS S2 (Workers in the Value Chain) governs disclosures about workers in the undertaking's **upstream and downstream value chain** under the CSRD — workers who are not employed by the undertaking itself but whose conditions are connected to the undertaking through its business relationships. This includes supply-chain factory workers, contracted logistics drivers, downstream service workers, and others.

S2 is the social standard most directly tied to **supply-chain due diligence** and **modern-slavery / human-rights** risk. It complements EU regulations like the Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CS3D) and the EU Forced Labour Regulation.

S2 is included in this wiki as a reference page; detailed workforce-conditions methodology is out of GHG-wiki scope.

(→ [[sources/esrs-set1-2023-2772|ESRS Set 1 — Commission Delegated Regulation 2023/2772]])

## Who Must Comply

CSRD reporters for whom **workers in the value chain are material** under the [[concepts/double-materiality|Double Materiality]] assessment. Often material for sectors with deep upstream supply chains: apparel, electronics, agriculture, construction, mining.

**Phase-in (size-conditional):** Undertakings ≤750 employees may omit *all* of ESRS S2 for the **first 2 years** of preparation (per [[concepts/esrs-phase-in|ESRS Phase-in]] / Appendix C of ESRS 1).

## Reporting Requirements

ESRS S2 contains 5 Disclosure Requirements:

| DR | Title |
|---|---|
| S2-1 | Policies related to value chain workers |
| S2-2 | Processes for engaging with value chain workers about impacts |
| S2-3 | Processes to remediate negative impacts and channels for value chain workers to raise concerns |
| S2-4 | Taking action on material impacts on value chain workers, and approaches to managing material risks and opportunities |
| S2-5 | Targets related to managing material negative impacts, advancing positive impacts, and managing material risks and opportunities |

Cross-cutting from ESRS 2: SBM-2 and SBM-3 with value-chain-worker-specific language.

## Deadlines

No standalone deadline. CSRD phased rollout applies. 2-year size-conditional relief for ≤750-employee undertakings.

## Penalties

Set by Member State CSRD transposition.

## Calculation Requirements

S2 is largely qualitative (process and policy disclosures) plus targets. Out of GHG-wiki scope.
