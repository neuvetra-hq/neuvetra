## Overview

ESRS S1 (Own Workforce) is the topical social standard governing disclosures about the undertaking's **own workforce** under the CSRD. It covers employees and non-employee workers in own operations (employees of contractors working at the undertaking's site, agency workers, self-employed people whose work is controlled by the undertaking).

S1 sits **outside the GHG/climate scope of this wiki**, but is included as a reference page so the wiki captures the full CSRD disclosure surface. The standard contains 17 Disclosure Requirements — the most of any topical ESRS — covering working conditions, equal treatment and opportunities, and other work-related rights including human rights.

For chatbot users asking about the social pillar of CSRD, this page anchors the topic. Detailed working-conditions methodology is out of scope for the GHG knowledge base.

(→ [[sources/esrs-set1-2023-2772|ESRS Set 1 — Commission Delegated Regulation 2023/2772]])

## Who Must Comply

CSRD reporters for whom **own workforce is material** under the [[concepts/double-materiality|Double Materiality]] assessment. Material for the vast majority of in-scope undertakings given that all employers necessarily have workforce-related impacts.

**Phase-in (size-conditional):** Undertakings ≤750 employees may omit *all* of ESRS S1 for the **first year** of preparation (per [[concepts/esrs-phase-in|ESRS Phase-in]] / Appendix C of ESRS 1).

## Reporting Requirements

ESRS S1 contains 17 Disclosure Requirements grouped into Strategy, IRO Management, and Metrics & Targets:

| DR | Title |
|---|---|
| S1-1 | Policies related to own workforce |
| S1-2 | Processes for engaging with own workforce and workers' representatives |
| S1-3 | Processes to remediate negative impacts and channels for own workforce to raise concerns |
| S1-4 | Taking action on material impacts on own workforce, and approaches to managing material risks and opportunities |
| S1-5 | Targets related to managing material negative impacts, advancing positive impacts, and managing material risks and opportunities |
| S1-6 | Characteristics of the undertaking's employees |
| S1-7 | Characteristics of non-employees in the undertaking's own workforce (first-year omission permitted for all DPs) |
| S1-8 | Collective bargaining coverage and social dialogue (first-year omission permitted for non-EEA employees) |
| S1-9 | Diversity metrics |
| S1-10 | Adequate wages |
| S1-11 | Social protection (first-year omission permitted) |
| S1-12 | Persons with disabilities — % of employees with disabilities (first-year omission permitted) |
| S1-13 | Training and skills development metrics (first-year omission permitted) |
| S1-14 | Health and safety metrics (selected datapoints + non-employee reporting first-year omission permitted) |
| S1-15 | Work-life balance metrics (first-year omission permitted) |
| S1-16 | Remuneration metrics (pay gap and total remuneration) |
| S1-17 | Incidents, complaints and severe human rights impacts |

Cross-cutting disclosures from [[regulations/esrs-2|ESRS 2]] also apply: SBM-2 (Interests and views of stakeholders) is augmented with S1-specific stakeholder-engagement language; SBM-3 (Material IROs and strategy interaction) is augmented with workforce-IRO specifics.

## Deadlines

No standalone deadline. CSRD phased rollout applies — see [[regulations/csrd|CSRD]]. First-year size-conditional and DR-specific reliefs apply per [[concepts/esrs-phase-in|ESRS Phase-in]].

## Penalties

Set by Member State CSRD transposition.

## Calculation Requirements

Workforce metrics use a mix of **headcount, FTE-equivalent, and percentage** units. Quantitative datapoints follow MDR-M structure. Out of GHG-wiki scope; refer to ESRS S1 Annex / Appendix in the source PDF for full methodology.
