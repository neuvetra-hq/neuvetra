## Overview

ESRS S4 (Consumers and End-Users) governs disclosures about impacts on **consumers and end-users** of the undertaking's products and services. Three impact axes structure the standard:

1. **Information-related impacts** — privacy, freedom of expression, access to quality information
2. **Personal safety impacts** — health and safety of consumers, security, child protection
3. **Social inclusion impacts** — non-discrimination, access to products/services, responsible marketing

S4 is included in this wiki as a reference page; detailed consumer-protection methodology is out of GHG-wiki scope.

(→ [[sources/esrs-set1-2023-2772|ESRS Set 1 — Commission Delegated Regulation 2023/2772]])

## Who Must Comply

CSRD reporters for whom **consumers and end-users are material** under the [[concepts/double-materiality|Double Materiality]] assessment. High-typical-materiality sectors: technology platforms, social media, healthcare, financial services (consumer-facing), pharmaceuticals, food & beverage, children's products, gambling.

**Phase-in (size-conditional):** Undertakings ≤750 employees may omit *all* of ESRS S4 for the **first 2 years** of preparation.

## Reporting Requirements

ESRS S4 contains 5 Disclosure Requirements:

| DR | Title |
|---|---|
| S4-1 | Policies related to consumers and end-users |
| S4-2 | Processes for engaging with consumers and end-users about impacts |
| S4-3 | Processes to remediate negative impacts and channels for consumers and end-users to raise concerns |
| S4-4 | Taking action on material impacts on consumers and end-users, and approaches to managing material risks and opportunities |
| S4-5 | Targets related to managing material negative impacts, advancing positive impacts, and managing material risks and opportunities |

Cross-cutting from ESRS 2: SBM-2 and SBM-3 with consumer-specific language.

## Deadlines

No standalone deadline. CSRD phased rollout applies. 2-year size-conditional relief for ≤750-employee undertakings.

## Penalties

Set by Member State CSRD transposition.

## Calculation Requirements

Largely qualitative. Where quantitative metrics are reported (incidents, complaints, recalls, breaches), MDR-M applies. Out of GHG-wiki scope.
