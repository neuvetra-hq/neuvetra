## Overview

ESRS E5 (Resource Use and Circular Economy) is the topical environmental standard governing material flow disclosures under the CSRD. It frames sustainability through the lens of the **circular economy** — extending the focus beyond waste management to cover the full **resource inflow / product / outflow / waste** lifecycle. The standard aligns with the **EU Circular Economy Action Plan** and the **EU Critical Raw Materials Act**.

E5 is structured around **resource inflows** (E5-4) and **resource outflows including waste** (E5-5), with policies, actions, targets, and financial effects covered separately. The **decoupling of growth from resource use** is the strategic narrative the standard expects undertakings to engage with.

(→ [[sources/esrs-set1-2023-2772|ESRS Set 1 — Commission Delegated Regulation 2023/2772]])

## Who Must Comply

CSRD reporters for whom **resource use and circular economy is material** under the [[concepts/double-materiality|Double Materiality]] assessment. High-typical-materiality sectors: manufacturing (especially electronics, automotive, construction materials), packaging, retail (extended producer responsibility), waste management, mining, agriculture (biological resource flows), construction & demolition.

For asset-light services with minimal material flows, resource-use can legitimately be non-material; all E5 disclosures may be omitted with a brief explanation.

## Reporting Requirements

### IRO-1 (E5-specific) — Process to identify material resource use and circular economy-related IROs
How the undertaking screens its operations and value chain for material material-flow IROs, with attention to critical raw materials, technical and biological cycles, and the design phase (where material decisions are locked in).

### E5-1 — Policies related to resource use and circular economy
Policies in the structure of ESRS 2 MDR-P. Includes circular design policies, secondary material sourcing, waste reduction, repair/reuse/refurbish commitments, alignment with extended producer responsibility (EPR) frameworks where applicable.

### E5-2 — Actions and resources related to resource use and circular economy
Action plans in the structure of ESRS 2 MDR-A. Includes design-for-circularity initiatives, take-back schemes, secondary-material supply development, partnerships with reuse/recycling infrastructure.

### E5-3 — Targets related to resource use and circular economy
Targets in the structure of ESRS 2 MDR-T. Common targets: % secondary content in inputs, % product recyclability, absolute waste reduction, virgin-material reduction, takeback program participation.

### E5-4 — Resource inflows
**Quantitative metrics:**
- **Total weight of products and technical and biological materials used** during the reporting period (in tonnes or kilograms)
- Breakdown of the weights of materials by type (e.g., metals, plastics, composites, biomass)
- Percentage of biological materials and biofuels sustainably sourced
- Percentage of secondary reused or recycled components, secondary intermediary products, and secondary materials used to manufacture the undertaking's products and services
- Where critical raw materials are material: weight of critical raw materials used

Reported under MDR-M structure.

### E5-5 — Resource outflows related to products and services and waste
**Quantitative metrics:**
- **Resource outflows / products and materials**: design-for-circularity attributes (durability, repairability, recyclability), expected product lifetime, % of mass recoverable
- **Waste**: total waste generated (tonnes), broken down by:
  - Hazardous vs non-hazardous
  - Composition / waste streams
  - Disposal route (landfill, incineration with/without energy recovery, recycling, reuse, composting)
  - **Total waste diverted from disposal** (recycling + reuse + composting)
  - **Total waste directed to disposal** (landfill + incineration)
- Total non-recycled waste

Reported under MDR-M structure.

### E5-6 — Anticipated financial effects from material resource use and circular economy-related impacts, risks and opportunities
- Material risks (raw material supply disruption, regulatory tightening on waste, EPR fees, critical raw material exposure)
- Material opportunities (circular product lines, reuse/refurbishment revenue, secondary materials trading)
- **Subject to phase-in:** first-year omission permitted; first 3 years qualitative-only reporting permitted
- See [[concepts/esrs-phase-in|ESRS Phase-in]]

Set by Member State CSRD transposition.

## Calculation Requirements

E5 metrics are in **mass units** (tonnes or kilograms). Mass-balance reconciliation between inflows (E5-4) and outflows + waste (E5-5) is implied by the standard but not strictly required at the disclosure level — the two are reported independently.

For **secondary content** (E5-4), the percentage may be measured by:
- Mass-balance allocation (when audited chain-of-custody is in place)
- Direct physical content measurement
- Whichever method is used must be disclosed under MDR-M

**Critical raw materials** disclosure references the EU Critical Raw Materials Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1252) list of strategic and critical raw materials.

For undertakings reporting both E1 and E5, **material flows often have associated GHG emissions**: secondary content typically reduces upstream Scope 3 Cat 1; waste-to-landfill drives Scope 3 Cat 5 emissions; waste-to-energy can shift emissions between Scope 3 and Scope 1. These linkages should be reflected in narrative even though the metrics are reported in different standards.
