## Overview

ESRS G1 (Business Conduct) is the topical governance standard governing disclosures about the undertaking's **business ethics and conduct** under the CSRD. It is the only governance topical standard in the first ESRS set. G1 covers corporate culture, supplier-relationship management, anti-corruption, anti-bribery, lobbying / political influence, and payment practices toward suppliers (especially SMEs).

G1 is included in this wiki as a reference page; detailed compliance / governance methodology is out of GHG-wiki scope, though G1-3 (corruption prevention) and G1-4 (corruption incidents) are common audit committee discussion topics for any large EU reporter.

(→ [[sources/esrs-set1-2023-2772|ESRS Set 1 — Commission Delegated Regulation 2023/2772]])

## Who Must Comply

CSRD reporters for whom **business conduct is material** under the [[concepts/double-materiality|Double Materiality]] assessment. Material for the vast majority of in-scope undertakings — corruption risk, lobbying, and payment practices apply broadly. No size-conditional first-year relief specific to G1 (unlike most social standards), so G1 typically applies in year 1.

## Reporting Requirements

ESRS G1 contains 6 Disclosure Requirements:

| DR | Title |
|---|---|
| G1-1 | Business conduct policies and corporate culture |
| G1-2 | Management of relationships with suppliers (incl. payment practices) |
| G1-3 | Prevention and detection of corruption and bribery |
| G1-4 | Incidents of corruption or bribery |
| G1-5 | Political influence and lobbying activities |
| G1-6 | Payment practices (toward suppliers, particularly SMEs) |

Cross-cutting from ESRS 2: GOV-1 (board oversight) is augmented with business-conduct-specific language.

## Deadlines

No standalone deadline. CSRD phased rollout applies — see [[regulations/csrd|CSRD]].

## Penalties

Set by Member State CSRD transposition. Note: corruption / bribery incidents themselves are subject to separate criminal and administrative law in each Member State; ESRS G1 governs *disclosure*, not the underlying conduct rules.

## Calculation Requirements

Mix of qualitative (policies, processes, culture) and quantitative (G1-4 incidents — number of cases, fines, dismissals; G1-5 political contribution amounts; G1-6 average days to pay suppliers, % of late payments). MDR-M applies. Out of GHG-wiki scope.
