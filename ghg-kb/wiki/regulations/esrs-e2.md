## Overview

ESRS E2 (Pollution) is the topical environmental standard governing pollution-related disclosures under the CSRD. It addresses pollution **to air, water, and soil**; pollution related to **living organisms**; and **substances of concern** — both regular substances of concern (SoC) and substances of very high concern (SVHC). Microplastics are explicitly included.

ESRS E2 sits alongside the climate standard ([[regulations/esrs-e1|ESRS E1]]) within the environmental topical block. While E1 is GHG-centric, E2 captures non-GHG environmental emissions and chemical pollution. For chemicals manufacturers, water-discharge-intensive operators, and any undertaking with hazardous-substance value chains, E2 is often co-material with E1.

E2 is structured around the four ESRS reporting areas (governance and strategy disclosures plug into [[regulations/esrs-2|ESRS 2]] cross-cutting requirements), plus six topic-specific Disclosure Requirements:

(→ [[sources/esrs-set1-2023-2772|ESRS Set 1 — Commission Delegated Regulation 2023/2772]])

## Who Must Comply

ESRS E2 applies to all CSRD reporters for whom **pollution is material** under the [[concepts/double-materiality|Double Materiality]] assessment. Sectors with high typical materiality include manufacturing (especially chemicals, metals, pulp & paper), energy & utilities, agriculture (pesticides/fertilizers), mining, and waste. Light-asset service companies will frequently conclude pollution is non-material.

**No "always-mandatory" disclosure equivalent to E1-1 transition plan** — if pollution is non-material, all of E2 may be omitted. ESRS 1 paragraph 32 special climate rule does not extend to E2; a brief explanation of non-materiality suffices.

## Reporting Requirements

### IRO-1 (E2-specific) — Process to identify material pollution-related IROs
How the undertaking screens its operations and value chain for material pollution impacts/risks/opportunities. Layered on top of generic [[regulations/esrs-2|ESRS 2 IRO-1]].

### E2-1 — Policies related to pollution
Policies adopted to manage material pollution matters. Reported in the structure of ESRS 2 MDR-P. Covers air, water, soil, living organisms, substances of concern, microplastics.

### E2-2 — Actions and resources related to pollution
Action plans and resources in the structure of ESRS 2 MDR-A. Includes prevention/mitigation/remediation actions, OpEx + CapEx, time horizons.

### E2-3 — Targets related to pollution
Targets in the structure of ESRS 2 MDR-T. Covers reduction targets for pollutants released to air, water, and soil; substance-of-concern phase-outs; alignment with EU Zero Pollution Action Plan or other policy frameworks where applicable.

### E2-4 — Pollution of air, water and soil
**Quantitative metrics:**
- Pollutants emitted to **air** (covered by EEA E-PRTR list and equivalent: NOx, SOx, NMVOC, NH3, particulate matter, heavy metals, etc.)
- Pollutants emitted to **water** (E-PRTR list: nitrogen, phosphorus, heavy metals, pesticides, etc.)
- Pollutants emitted to **soil** (E-PRTR list)
- **Microplastics** generated and/or used by the undertaking
- For each pollutant: amount, breakdown by significant region/site where material

Reported under MDR-M structure (methodology, data sources, comparability).

### E2-5 — Substances of concern and substances of very high concern
- **SoC**: amounts of substances of concern that are generated/used/produced/sold (per the EU REACH framework + CLP Regulation criteria)
- **SVHC**: amounts of substances of very high concern (REACH Annex XIV candidate list / authorization list) — generated/used/produced/sold
- Breakdown by hazard class and hazard category where material

### E2-6 — Anticipated financial effects from material pollution-related impacts, risks and opportunities
- Material risks: financial exposure to regulatory tightening, remediation liabilities, asset impairments tied to pollution
- Material opportunities: revenue from low-emission technologies, products supporting circular economy
- Financial effects on the undertaking's financial position, performance, and cash flows
- **Subject to phase-in:** first-year omission permitted; first 3 years qualitative-only reporting permitted (except paragraph 40(b) on operating/capital expenditures incurred in connection with major incidents and deposits, which is required from year 1)
- See [[concepts/esrs-phase-in|ESRS Phase-in]]

## Deadlines

No standalone deadline. Effective with the CSRD phased rollout for in-scope companies starting FY 2024 — see [[regulations/csrd|CSRD]] for company-level dates.

## Penalties

Set by Member State CSRD transposition; no standalone penalty regime.

## Calculation Requirements

ESRS E2 references the **EU E-PRTR (European Pollutant Release and Transfer Register)** list as the canonical pollutant taxonomy. Undertakings should align E2-4 metrics with their existing E-PRTR reporting obligations where applicable. Microplastics methodology references guidance from ECHA (European Chemicals Agency).

For substances of concern (E2-5), the legal basis is the EU REACH Regulation (EC) 1907/2006 and the CLP Regulation (EC) 1272/2008.

Pollution emissions are reported in **mass units** (typically tonnes/year or kilograms/year for trace pollutants). Microplastics in tonnes/year. No GHG-equivalence conversion is applied — pollution disclosures are independent of GHG metrics.
