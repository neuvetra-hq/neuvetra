## Overview

ESRS S3 (Affected Communities) governs disclosures about the undertaking's actual and potential impacts on **affected communities** — particularly indigenous peoples, local communities adjacent to operations, and communities affected through the undertaking's value chain. Material impacts often relate to land rights, free prior and informed consent (FPIC), economic/social/cultural rights, security forces, and access to remedy.

S3 is the social standard most relevant for extractive industries, large infrastructure, agribusiness, and energy projects. It is included in this wiki as a reference page; detailed community-engagement methodology is out of GHG-wiki scope, although **just transition** considerations link S3 to E1 transition planning.

(→ [[sources/esrs-set1-2023-2772|ESRS Set 1 — Commission Delegated Regulation 2023/2772]])

## Who Must Comply

CSRD reporters for whom **affected communities are material** under the [[concepts/double-materiality|Double Materiality]] assessment. High-typical-materiality sectors: extractive industries (oil & gas, mining), agribusiness, large infrastructure, hydropower, real-estate development on contested land.

**Phase-in (size-conditional):** Undertakings ≤750 employees may omit *all* of ESRS S3 for the **first 2 years** of preparation.

## Reporting Requirements

ESRS S3 contains 5 Disclosure Requirements:

| DR | Title |
|---|---|
| S3-1 | Policies related to affected communities |
| S3-2 | Processes for engaging with affected communities about impacts |
| S3-3 | Processes to remediate negative impacts and channels for affected communities to raise concerns |
| S3-4 | Taking action on material impacts on affected communities, and approaches to managing material risks and opportunities |
| S3-5 | Targets related to managing material negative impacts, advancing positive impacts, and managing material risks and opportunities |

Cross-cutting from ESRS 2: SBM-2 and SBM-3 with affected-communities-specific language.

## Deadlines

No standalone deadline. CSRD phased rollout applies. 2-year size-conditional relief for ≤750-employee undertakings.

## Penalties

Set by Member State CSRD transposition.

## Calculation Requirements

Largely qualitative. Where quantitative metrics are reported (number of incidents, FPIC consultations, grievance mechanism cases), MDR-M applies. Out of GHG-wiki scope.
