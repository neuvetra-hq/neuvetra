## Overview

ESRS 2 (General Disclosures) is the cross-cutting standard that contains the **disclosure requirements applicable across all material sustainability matters**, regardless of which topical standard those matters fall under. It is the connective tissue between ESRS 1 (which sets the rules) and the topical standards (which set topic-specific disclosures). Every CSRD reporter must apply ESRS 2 in full — there is no materiality test that can switch ESRS 2 off.

ESRS 2 organizes disclosures into the four ESRS reporting areas:

- **Governance (GOV)** — board / management oversight of sustainability matters
- **Strategy (SBM)** — interaction of strategy and business model with material impacts, risks, opportunities
- **Impact, risk and opportunity management (IRO)** — the materiality assessment process and how policies/actions/targets/metrics are managed
- **Metrics and targets (MT)** — embedded in topical standards but governed by ESRS 2 Minimum Disclosure Requirements

Plus a **Basis for Preparation (BP)** section covering the general accounting basis of the sustainability statement, and four cross-cutting **Minimum Disclosure Requirements (MDRs)** that structure how policies, actions, metrics, and targets are presented within every topical standard.

(→ [[sources/esrs-set1-2023-2772|ESRS Set 1 — Commission Delegated Regulation 2023/2772]])

## Who Must Comply

Every undertaking in scope of CSRD (→ [[regulations/csrd|CSRD]]). All ESRS 2 disclosures are mandatory regardless of materiality assessment outcomes — they are the floor below which no CSRD report can fall. Topical standards layer on top of ESRS 2 only for *material* topics.

## Reporting Requirements

### Basis for Preparation

| DR | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **BP-1** | General basis for preparation of the sustainability statement | Reporting boundary (consolidation), consistency with financial statements, omitted disclosures with justification |
| **BP-2** | Disclosures in relation to specific circumstances | Use of transitional provisions, departure from medium/long-term horizons, classified information omissions, prior-period error corrections, source-of-estimation disclosures |

### Governance

| DR | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **GOV-1** | The role of the administrative, management and supervisory bodies | Body composition, expertise on sustainability, oversight responsibilities, executive vs non-executive split, gender diversity |
| **GOV-2** | Information provided to and sustainability matters addressed by the administrative, management and supervisory bodies | How and how often the board is informed about IROs, policy implementation, due diligence results; list of material IROs addressed |
| **GOV-3** | Integration of sustainability-related performance in incentive schemes | Sustainability metrics in remuneration, % of variable pay tied to sustainability, approval level |
| **GOV-4** | Statement on due diligence | Mapping of how the OECD/UN Guiding Principles due-diligence aspects are reflected throughout the sustainability statement |
| **GOV-5** | Risk management and internal controls over sustainability reporting | RM/IC scope, risk assessment approach, main risks identified, integration into reporting |

### Strategy

| DR | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **SBM-1** | Strategy, business model and value chain | Significant products/services, markets, headcount by geography, **revenue breakdown by significant ESRS sector** (deferred until sector classification delegated act), exposure to fossil fuel / chemicals / controversial weapons / tobacco, sustainability goals, business-model description, value chain main features |
| **SBM-2** | Interests and views of stakeholders | Key stakeholders engaged, engagement processes, how outcomes inform strategy, board awareness of stakeholder views |
| **SBM-3** | Material impacts, risks and opportunities and their interaction with strategy and business model | Outcome of materiality assessment: material IROs by topic, time horizons, value-chain location, financial effects (anticipated financial effects subject to first-year and 3-year qualitative-only relief) |

### Impact, Risk and Opportunity Management

| DR | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **IRO-1** | Description of the process to identify and assess material impacts, risks and opportunities | The DMA process, methodology, threshold-setting, stakeholder input, scenario use; topical IRO-1 layers on top with topic-specific extensions. Operationalised by [[sources/efrag-ig1-materiality-2024\|EFRAG IG 1]] — see [[methodologies/double-materiality-assessment\|Double Materiality Assessment]] |
| **IRO-2** | Disclosure Requirements in ESRS covered by the undertaking's sustainability statement | List of all DRs in scope (including phased-in, omitted, and entity-specific); for E1, the detailed climate-non-materiality justification (ESRS 1 paragraph 32) is reported under IRO-2. EFRAG IG 3 datapoint workbook ([[sources/efrag-ig3-datapoints-2024\|IG 3]]) is the standard preparer reference for the IRO-2 inventory |

### Minimum Disclosure Requirements (cross-cutting)

These four Minimum Disclosure Requirements apply jointly with each topical standard's policy, action, metric, and target disclosures. They give the report a consistent shape across topics.

| MDR | Title | What it requires |
|---|---|---|
| **MDR-P** | Policies adopted to manage material sustainability matters | Each policy: scope of matters covered, scope (own ops vs value chain), most senior accountable level, third-party standards referenced, stakeholder considerations, availability and communication |
| **MDR-A** | Actions and resources in relation to material sustainability matters | Each action plan: scope, time horizon, expected outcomes, OpEx and CapEx allocated, resources (financial + non-financial) needed and available |
| **MDR-M** | Metrics in relation to material sustainability matters | Methodologies and assumptions, data sources, validation approach, comparability over time, restatements |
| **MDR-T** | Tracking effectiveness of policies and actions through targets | For each target: scope, baseline, milestone years, methodology, measurement frequency, alignment with science-based or regulatory references where relevant |

The MDRs are how policies/actions/metrics/targets are reported under E1, E2, E3, E4, E5, S1, S2, S3, S4, G1. For example, a climate target reported under ESRS E1-4 must be presented in the structure mandated by MDR-T.

## Deadlines

No standalone deadlines. ESRS 2 follows the CSRD phased rollout — see [[regulations/csrd|CSRD]] for company-level effective dates.

## Penalties

No standalone penalties; failure to apply ESRS 2 invalidates the sustainability statement under CSRD. Penalty amounts are set by Member State transposition.

## Calculation Requirements

ESRS 2 itself contains no quantitative metrics. Its quantitative role is in **structuring how topical metrics are presented** (via MDR-M) and in **anticipated financial effects** (SBM-3 paragraph 48(e)). For the latter:

**Datapoint inventory (per EFRAG IG 3, May 2024):** the full ESRS Set 1 contains roughly **161 datapoints mandatory irrespective of materiality** (covering all of ESRS 2 and each topical standard's IRO-1), **622 datapoints subject to materiality** (topical DRs that apply only when the matter is material), and **269 voluntary "may disclose" datapoints**. The IG 3 Excel workbook lists each datapoint with ESRS reference, paragraph, data type, conditional / voluntary flags, SFDR / Taxonomy / Pillar 3 cross-references, and phase-in years (per ≤750-employee and per all undertakings). See (→ [[sources/efrag-ig3-datapoints-2024|EFRAG IG 3 — Datapoints]]).

- First-year: may be omitted entirely
- First 3 years: may be qualitative-only if quantitative measurement is impracticable

See [[concepts/esrs-phase-in|ESRS Phase-in and Transitional Provisions]] for full detail.

GOV-1 paragraph 21 includes one quantitative datapoint relevant to gender diversity that connects to SFDR Principal Adverse Impact indicator #13. This is a directly-coded EU regulatory link rather than a GHG metric.
