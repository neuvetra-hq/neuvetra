## Definition

ESRS phase-in (transitional) provisions are the relief mechanisms that allow undertakings in scope of CSRD to omit, defer, or qualitatively-only report certain disclosure requirements during the first 1–3 years of mandatory ESRS reporting. They are codified in **ESRS 1, Section 10 and Appendix C**, and apply on top of the CSRD scope-phasing (which staggers *which companies* must report by year). Phase-in provisions stagger *which disclosures within each report* are required immediately versus later.

Three categories of relief operate concurrently:

1. **Size-conditional reliefs** — keyed to the 750-employee threshold (consolidated headcount on the balance sheet date for the financial year)
2. **First-year reliefs** — apply to all undertakings in their first ESRS reporting year, regardless of size
3. **Three-year qualitative-only reliefs** — apply to "anticipated financial effects" disclosures across multiple topical standards, where quantitative measurement is impracticable

Plus a separate **value-chain transitional provision** (ESRS 1 paragraph 132 area, "Section 10.2") allowing undertakings to exclude metric-level value chain information not reasonably available for the first 3 reporting years, with disclosure of efforts.

## Why It Matters

Phase-in determines what a company must *actually* disclose in any given year — not what is theoretically required by the standards. For a typical large EU undertaking entering CSRD reporting in FY 2024 or FY 2025, the practical reporting obligation in year 1 is materially smaller than the full ESRS catalog suggests. Misreading phase-in is one of the most common preparer errors, and is the area where CSRD compliance advisors most often need to give precise guidance.

For the chatbot, phase-in questions ("do I have to report Scope 3 in my first year?", "can my client skip ESRS S1 if they're under 750 employees?") are frequent and high-stakes. Centralizing the rules here keeps every topical regulation page from having to repeat them.

## Key Distinctions

### Size-conditional reliefs (750-employee threshold)

Undertakings or groups whose **average number of employees during the financial year** does not exceed **750 on the balance sheet date** (consolidated where applicable) may omit certain disclosures:

| Standard / DR | Relief | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| ESRS E1-6 (Scope 3 + Total GHG datapoints) | May omit | First year only |
| ESRS E4 (all disclosure requirements) | May omit entirely | First 2 years |
| ESRS S1 (all disclosure requirements) | May omit entirely | First year only |
| ESRS S2 (all disclosure requirements) | May omit entirely | First 2 years |
| ESRS S3 (all disclosure requirements) | May omit entirely | First 2 years |
| ESRS S4 (all disclosure requirements) | May omit entirely | First 2 years |

Rationale: smaller in-scope undertakings (often listed SMEs, mid-size groups) get more time to build the data infrastructure for value-chain-heavy disclosures.

### First-year reliefs (regardless of size)

The following may be omitted entirely in the **first year of preparation** of the sustainability statement, by any in-scope undertaking:

| Standard / DR | What may be omitted |
|---|---|
| ESRS E1-9 | All "anticipated financial effects" datapoints (climate physical and transition risks) |
| ESRS E2-6 | All "anticipated financial effects" datapoints (pollution-related) |
| ESRS E3-5 | All "anticipated financial effects" datapoints (water and marine resources) |
| ESRS E4-6 | All "anticipated financial effects" datapoints (biodiversity-related) |
| ESRS E5-6 | All "anticipated financial effects" datapoints (resource use and circular economy) |
| ESRS S1-7 | Characteristics of non-employee workers in own workforce (all datapoints) |
| ESRS S1-8 | Collective bargaining coverage (with regard to non-EEA employees) |
| ESRS S1-11 | Social protection |
| ESRS S1-12 | Persons with disabilities |
| ESRS S1-13 | Training and skills development metrics |
| ESRS S1-14 | Health & safety: cases of work-related ill-health, days lost to injuries/accidents/fatalities/ill-health datapoints AND non-employee reporting |
| ESRS S1-15 | Work-life balance |
| ESRS 2 SBM-3 paragraph 48(e) | Anticipated financial effects |

### Three-year qualitative-only reliefs

For the first **3 years** of preparation of the sustainability statement, where it is **impracticable to prepare quantitative disclosures**, the undertaking may comply by reporting **only qualitative** disclosures for the following:

- ESRS 2 SBM-3 paragraph 48(e) — anticipated financial effects from material impacts, risks and opportunities
- ESRS E1-9 — anticipated financial effects from material physical and transition risks and potential climate-related opportunities
- ESRS E2-6 — anticipated financial effects from material pollution-related impacts, risks and opportunities (except paragraph 40(b) operating/capital expenditures linked to major incidents)
- ESRS E3-5 — anticipated financial effects from material water and marine resources-related impacts, risks and opportunities
- ESRS E4-6 — anticipated financial effects from material biodiversity and ecosystem-related impacts, risks and opportunities
- ESRS E5-6 — anticipated financial effects from material resource use and circular economy-related impacts, risks and opportunities

### Value-chain transitional provision (ESRS 1 Section 10.2)

For the first **3 years** of preparation of the sustainability statement, an undertaking may **exclude metric-level upstream/downstream value chain information** that is not reasonably available, provided it discloses:

- The efforts it has made to obtain the information
- The reasons why all the necessary information could not be obtained
- The plans to obtain the information in the future

This applies to *quantitative* metrics that depend on value chain data, including elements of Scope 3 emissions where direct collection is impractical. Even within the transitional relief, sector-average data and other proxies remain the preferred fallback over outright exclusion (see ESRS 1 chapter 5).

**EFRAG IG 2 operationalisation (§2.7–2.8, ¶22–24):** Year 1 may be **qualitative-only** for value-chain metrics where direct data collection is genuinely impracticable; qualitative descriptions of value-chain IROs are required from year 1 regardless. "Reasonable effort" is **proportional** — there is no expectation to quantify impacts of thousands of tier-3 suppliers, but tier-1 major suppliers and customers must be engaged (IG 2 ¶160). The undertaking should disclose basis, accuracy level, and planned improvements (ESRS 2 ¶10).

**LSME cap (IG 2 ¶94):** The undertaking cannot require value-chain SMEs to provide data beyond what future LSME standards will require — limiting compliance push-down to the value chain. LSME standards under development by EFRAG; finalisation expected end-2024.

(→ [[sources/efrag-ig2-value-chain-2024|EFRAG IG 2 — Value Chain (May 2024)]])

### Sector-revenue-breakdown deferral (SBM-1)

ESRS 2 SBM-1 paragraph 40(b) (breakdown of total revenue by significant ESRS sector) and 40(c) (list of additional significant ESRS sectors) are deferred until the application date specified in a future Commission Delegated Act on the ESRS sector classification. As of the date of this regulation, that delegated act has not been adopted; the disclosure is therefore not yet operative.

### Comparative information transitional provision (ESRS 1 Section 10.3)

In the first year of preparation, comparative prior-year data may be omitted for most quantitative metrics where retrospective reconstruction is impracticable.

### Entity-specific disclosure transitional provision (ESRS 1 Section 10.1)

The requirement to provide entity-specific disclosures for material matters not covered by an ESRS may be omitted in the first year of preparation.

## Calculation Notes

Phase-in interacts with materiality:

- A relief allows omission of *otherwise-required* disclosures. A topic that is not material is omitted regardless of phase-in (ESRS does not mandate disclosure on non-material topics, except the special climate-change-non-material justification rule of ESRS 1 paragraph 32).
- For E1-6, the 750-employee Scope 3 omission is independent of materiality. Even if Scope 3 is material, an undertaking ≤750 employees may omit it in year 1.
- Use of any phase-in must be disclosed transparently — under ESRS 2 BP-2 (Disclosures in relation to specific circumstances) where the undertaking is using a transitional provision.
