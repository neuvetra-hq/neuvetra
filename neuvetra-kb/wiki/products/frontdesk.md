# FrontDesk — AI Receptionist

## What It Is

FrontDesk is an **AI receptionist that answers your business phone**. It picks up incoming calls 24/7, has a real conversation with the caller, books appointments, and routes anything it can't handle to you.

It runs as a subscription. You don't configure it like a phone tree, train it like a hire, or build it like a platform — you sign up, point your number at it, and it starts working.

## Who It's For

Small and mid-sized businesses where the phone is the primary way customers ask questions and book — and where missing those calls (after hours, mid-job, mid-meeting) means missing the work.

Common fits:
- Service trades — auto repair shops, cleaning companies, dealerships
- Health practices — chiropractic, dental, small clinics
- Professional services — accounting and bookkeeping firms
- Anywhere a small team can't keep someone on the phones without sacrificing the actual job

## How It Works

1. You sign up and tell FrontDesk what your business does, who books with you, and what your hours and services are.
2. You forward your number to FrontDesk (or use a number we provide).
3. Calls come in. FrontDesk answers, listens, asks the right follow-up questions, and books the caller into your calendar.
4. Anything outside its remit — a complaint, an unusual request, a regular it should know — gets handed back to you with a summary.

## Key Features

- **24/7 answering.** Never a missed call, including nights and weekends.
- **Calendar booking.** Connects to Google, Outlook, or any CalDAV calendar; books directly into your existing schedule.
- **Caller summaries.** A short text or email after each call so you know what happened without listening to a recording.
- **Industry-aware.** Trained for the conversation patterns common to your industry, not a generic phone tree.
- **Handoff to you.** Anything FrontDesk can't or shouldn't handle gets surfaced to you immediately.
