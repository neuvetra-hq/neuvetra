# Terrascope — AI Emissions Analyst

## What It Is

Terrascope is an **AI emissions analyst that calculates your greenhouse-gas footprint and files the regulatory report for you**. It walks you through what counts as Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions for your business, gathers the right data, runs the math, and produces a report ready to file under the rules that apply to you.

It runs as a subscription. You don't have to learn the GHG Protocol, hire a sustainability consultant, or build a carbon-accounting spreadsheet — you answer the questions Terrascope asks, and it produces the deliverable.

## Who It's For

Businesses that need to file a climate or emissions report under a regulation, but don't have a dedicated sustainability team. Today that primarily means:

- **California.** Companies subject to **SB 253** (emissions disclosure for large in-state operators) or **SB 261** (climate-related financial-risk disclosure for mid-revenue companies). Also **CARB Mandatory Reporting** for facility-level filers.
- **European Union.** Companies in scope of **CSRD / ESRS E1** climate reporting, or covered by the **EU ETS**.
- **Voluntary disclosures.** Businesses preparing TCFD-style or investor-facing climate statements ahead of formal mandates.

Coverage expands as more jurisdictions adopt mandatory disclosure rules.

## How It Works

1. You tell Terrascope what your business does, where it operates, and which rules you're filing under.
2. Terrascope guides you through the data it needs — fuel use, electricity, refrigerants, business travel, supply-chain spend, and the other Scope 1/2/3 inputs that apply to your operations.
3. It runs the calculations using the methodology required by your filing (GHG Protocol, IPCC factors, jurisdiction-specific overlays).
4. It produces a filing-ready report and walks you through what to attach where.

## Key Features

- **Scope 1, 2, and 3 coverage.** Direct emissions, purchased energy, and the value-chain categories that apply to your business.
- **Jurisdiction-aware.** Knows the differences between California (SB 253, SB 261, CARB MRR) and EU (CSRD, ESRS E1, EU ETS) reporting requirements; uses the right methodology for each.
- **Conversational data collection.** Asks for data the way a human analyst would, not a 200-row spreadsheet.
- **Filing-ready output.** Reports formatted to the structure each regulation expects.
- **Numbers come from a calculator, not the AI.** Every emission figure Terrascope shows you originates from a deterministic calculation against published emission factors — not from a language model guessing.
