# Neuvetra — overview

## What It Is

Neuvetra is **AI specialists for every job in your business**. Each one is a finished tool — not a platform you have to set up. You subscribe, and it gets to work.

Today there are two: **FrontDesk** answers your phones; **Terrascope** files your emissions reports. More are on the way.

## Who It's For

Small and mid-sized business owners. Specifically: owners running businesses where the work is happening — answering customers, processing paperwork, filing reports — and who'd rather subscribe to AI that does that work than hire it, build it, or configure it.

## How It Works

Pick the specialist that fits your business. Subscribe. It starts doing the job.

No platform to build. No AI to configure. No specialist to hire. The tool runs itself; you run the rest of the business.

## Key Features

The current specialists:

- **[[products/frontdesk|FrontDesk]]** — your AI receptionist. Answers calls, books appointments, never sleeps.
- **[[products/terrascope|Terrascope]]** — your AI emissions analyst. Calculates Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions and files reports under California (SB 253, SB 261, CARB MRR) and EU (CSRD, ESRS E1) regulations.

Each new specialist will own a single job for a single kind of business.

## Plans

Pricing is per specialist. See [[products/frontdesk|FrontDesk]] and [[products/terrascope|Terrascope]] for plans.
