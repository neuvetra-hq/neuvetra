## Tips
- Landscape orientation, at least 800x600px
- Real business photography works best (dentist at work, lawyer in office, HVAC tech on job site)
- The card shows the photo at h-52 (208px) with object-cover — center the subject
- If a file is missing, the card shows a colored gradient fallback automatically
