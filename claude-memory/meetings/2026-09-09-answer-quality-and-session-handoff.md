## Decisions

**Answer quality.** Context: accurate individual sentences were being mistaken for complete, useful answers. Options: add individual FAQ exceptions or strengthen whole-question interpretation and review. Call: use general condition/operation/task-fit checks, source-qualified immutable wording and independent outcome review. Why: the application must handle varied questions without hiding missing coverage. Consequence: passed offline checks are retained as engineering evidence; new live outcomes and a working browser demonstration are still required.

**Source scope.** Context: the proposed additional GHG Protocol corpus has no established hosted-use basis for commercial-product development. Options: proceed under an unsupported assumption, hold all work, or hold that source route while continuing approved EPA work. Coordinator/QA disposition: retain the new candidate locally and proceed with unaffected EPA engineering. Why: content fidelity, intended-use permission and runtime acceptance are distinct. Consequence: a concrete permission request is drafted but unsent; the wider 29-case gate remains held. This is an operational disposition, not a new claim that the board granted publisher rights.

**Execution reporting.** Context: the board expected ongoing agent work, while reports described tasks that had ended. Call: distinguish completed, queued, blocked and actually running assignments in every report. Why: role prompts, notes and queues do not create workers. Consequence: no always-on business or scheduled continuation is claimed; future implementation must be explicitly demonstrated. A source-specific hold must not become an unexplained pause of unrelated engineering.

**Next increment.** Context: the corrected engine can use the existing approved EPA release. Call: prepare and independently evaluate that narrower candidate, then demonstrate a matching website before resuming board testing. Why: it gives a concrete next step without misrepresenting broader source coverage. Consequence: preserve the unchanged 18 baseline expectations and separately assess three current-source feedback probes. Do not count their honest limitations as success for the held expanded positive-answer cases.
