# Connected inventory plan acceptance

Connect company, period, locations and activities to the seven-step intake; support different businesses without industry guesses; capture nonstandard sources and persist work.

- Acknowledgment saves onboarding and opens a plan generated from those answers; no example-company fallback.
- Five direct-source answers preserve Yes, No, uncertain and missing states. Stable location IDs, explicit multi-site assignment, visible orphan links.
- Versioned primary-source collection guidance, selectable equipment subtypes, four purchased-energy screens and all 15 Scope 3 categories; custom sources remain possible.
- Notes, activity records, evidence bytes and screening persist through reload/restart; history and revision checks prevent silent concurrent overwrites.
- Changed inputs trigger review; company/period change creates a fresh plan and preserves old work in history.
- Honest empty/error states, escaped content, local security boundaries and evidence limits.
- Independent integrated review and cross-industry/negative tests.

Acceptance is a working local collection workflow, not universal methods, released factors, production tenant readiness or assurance.
