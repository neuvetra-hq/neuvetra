# Model performance reviews

Board approved 2026-09-22. Owner: coordinator. Applies prospectively; existing product assignments keep their contracts. This policy does not start a scheduler or confer accounting accreditation.

## Staffing decisions

A role is a contract; its model is replaceable. Start with current capable candidates suited to the task, not the cheapest or oldest available model. Existing critical accounting, security and final QA settings remain until independently reviewed evidence supports a change. Candidate status is not employment or demonstrated competence.

Each role/model pairing has a versioned decision: candidate, probation, retained, restricted or replaced. Record requested and observed model/effort, task class, evaluation set version, sample size, first-review acceptance, unique/reopened findings by severity, review and repair effort, measured API dollars, unknown measurement coverage, and decision evidence. Never rank unlike tasks as if they were comparable. Do not infer model identity from the role name.

Initial pilot: two capable coding candidates on the same bounded synthetic cases. Public model availability and provider prices must be refreshed before calls. The $5 is an aggregate ceiling, not a spending target. A small pilot establishes feasibility only, not suitability for autonomous greenhouse-gas accounting. No production access, customer inputs or execution of generated code is necessary for this first screening.

## Decisions and safeguards

- A critical unsupported accounting claim, authorization violation or hidden failed check suspends that candidate from the affected responsibility pending independent diagnosis. Preserve the output.
- Two failed material repairs trigger diagnosis and a stronger worker or better task contract. Infrastructure/provider failures are tracked separately from demonstrated reasoning defects.
- After at least five comparable independently reviewed assignments, consider probationary placement. Report the raw denominator; five cases are not statistical proof. Full replacement requires a subsequent fresh evaluation and no unresolved material findings.
- Retain a candidate only when correctness gates pass and total delivery cost, including review and repair, is acceptable. Low token price never compensates for failed correctness gates.
- Keep a known working fallback. Change one role/task class at a time, record the previous model and rollback criteria, and verify the next three comparable assignments. Do not change global defaults from one successful example.

## Management cadence

At feature closure: generate the scorecard, record the independent verdict and one actionable lesson. At milestone closure: coordinator reviews the largest rework/cost drivers and reports staffing decisions and one or two process improvements. These are task-triggered reviews; no paid idle monitoring or scheduled model calls.

New roles receive a compact mission/authority contract, representative held-out cases, baseline model choice, named independent reviewer and probationary assignments before expanded responsibility. Lessons are tested on later variants; avoid copying every historical failure into every prompt.

Deterministic code remains responsible for calculations and metrics. Models may propose evidence mappings, tests and patches; they cannot approve their own work, change source release status or provide professional external assurance.
