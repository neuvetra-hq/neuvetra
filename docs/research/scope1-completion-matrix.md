## Initial supported corporate profile

The first supported Scope 1 profile is a non-industrial corporate-services and distribution company with:

- one California parent under operational control for one complete calendar reporting year;
- one California office with its gas boiler, HVAC and fire-suppression equipment, and one California distribution center with space heating, a fossil-diesel emergency generator, HVAC/refrigeration and fire-suppression equipment;
- an owned or operationally controlled on-road distribution fleet whose complete vehicle register must reconcile to source workpapers; M74's first fixture is one controlled diesel truck, but any additional discovered vehicle remains a gap until supported;
- no manufacturing, chemical transformation, mineral production, metal production, oil and gas production, waste treatment, agricultural production, or other process-emission activity, but only after an explicit process-source screen supports that conclusion;
- all operations inside the declared corporate boundary, including travel or fuel consumed outside California by a controlled vehicle, retained in the inventory; and
- no acquired/disposed entity, partial-year control, joint operation, shared or sub-metered fuel, biofuel/blend, renewable diesel, aircraft, marine vessel, rail or off-road equipment. Common office/distribution sources are never removed from the profile to fit existing methods: unsupported fire-suppression, HVAC, refrigeration, backup-power or vehicle facts remain blocking gaps.

The profile is an admission contract, not a shortcut to completeness. Discovery of another entity, facility, vehicle, combustion device, refrigerant-bearing asset, process source, reporting-period change, or ownership/control complication changes the result to `incomplete` or `unsupported` until that item is reconciled. A customer with an unsupported activity may still use supported workpapers, but Neuvetra must refuse a complete-Scope-1 claim.

M73 demonstrated only one synthetic California office boiler using annual 2025 natural gas, HHV MMBtu, and a dedicated meter. Its factor and method remain development candidates and unreleased. M73 is evidence of workflow behavior, persistence, recovery, and a bounded subtotal; it is not evidence that stationary combustion, Scope 1, or this profile is complete.

## Sequenced path to supported Scope 1

1. **M74 — owned heavy-duty diesel truck:** one stable, operationally controlled on-road distribution truck for calendar 2025. Require fossil-diesel gallons for CO2 and vehicle miles plus supported class/model-year facts for CH4/N2O. Exercise duplicate prevention and preserve all M73 history. This produces one mobile-source workpaper, not a fleet or Scope 1 total.
2. **M75 — mobile-fleet reconciliation:** reconcile the complete controlled-vehicle register and produce workpapers for every vehicle that fits the reviewed M74 profile. If another fuel, class, model year, mode, control arrangement or grouping is applicable, keep it blocking and add a separate method milestone.
3. **M76 — stationary-source completion for the admitted profile:** inventory the office and distribution-center stationary devices, complete natural-gas coverage and the fossil-diesel emergency generator only where evidence, factors, rights and deterministic methods pass review. Unsupported stationary fuels or shared/allocated meters remain blocking gaps.
4. **M77 — refrigerants and other fugitives:** reconcile office/distribution HVAC, warehouse refrigeration and fire-suppression equipment and service/discharge evidence with released methods. Unsupported gases or incomplete service histories block completion.
5. **M78 — process screen and corporate Scope 1 close:** support an evidence-backed `not applicable` process decision for this non-industrial profile, reconcile all source families, detect duplicates/overlaps, aggregate gross totals without offset netting, freeze reports/workpapers, and demonstrate correction, separate review, tenant isolation and recovery on the integrated version.
6. **Release and human gates:** source/method rights and production releases, current regulatory applicability, real-customer operational readiness and qualified independent human assurance handoff must still pass before a customer-facing supported/compliance/assurance claim.

Sequence numbers are planning boundaries, not promises that a method will pass review. If M74–M77 reveals an unsupported but applicable source, M78 must return `Scope 1 incomplete`; the team must add a bounded reviewed method milestone rather than redefine the customer profile after seeing the data.

## Completion decision

For the declared profile and reporting year, the product may label Scope 1 `supported and complete for the declared profile` only when:

1. the boundary and all entities/facilities/source families reconcile with no unresolved applicable source;
2. every included result uses released evidence/method/factor/GWP bindings and deterministic arithmetic;
3. all missing data, estimates, uncertainty, duplicates, overlaps and exclusions have a visible supported disposition;
4. the gross company total exactly reconciles to one effective version of every included source and contains no offset netting;
5. exact history, separate review, reports/workpapers, tenant controls, persistence and recovery pass integrated independent review; and
6. the claim text states the declared profile/year and does not imply SB 253 compliance, external assurance, filing acceptance or support for a company outside the admission contract.

Until then, the only permitted company-level state is `Scope 1 incomplete` with the blocking rows named. Unknown is never zero; absence of a process or refrigerant worksheet is never evidence of non-applicability.
