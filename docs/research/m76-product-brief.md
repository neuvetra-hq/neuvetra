# M76 — Stationary equipment and fuel coverage

The board accepted the M75 live browser demonstration and directed continued work on September 16, 2026. Root coordinates this California-first increment on the existing rolling PR. This brief defines product acceptance; it does not release accounting methods or establish complete Scope 1 coverage.

## Outcome

A company reviewer can compare an independently declared equipment list with the corporate source register and saved stationary workpapers. The demonstration covers an office gas boiler, a separately metered distribution-center gas space heater, and a fixed fossil-diesel emergency generator when the accounting contract supports its exact fuel and activity evidence. Every other declared or registered stationary source remains visible.

The equipment list must come from a separate discovery statement, including the facilities inspected and how equipment was identified. It cannot be generated from the workpapers and called complete. One admitted device has one dedicated fuel evidence relationship. Shared meters/tanks, fuel allocations, stock-derived consumption, blends, uncertain control, missing operating periods and unapproved device profiles remain blocking gaps.

## Demonstration and acceptance

1. Open stationary equipment coverage before entering its declaration. Existing corporate sources and workpapers appear as unresolved matches; an empty form never means zero sources.
2. Declare independently identified equipment, entities, facilities, full-year control, fuel, device identity and dedicated evidence relationships. Unknown facts remain unknown. Match each declaration row to its corporate source explicitly.
3. Reuse the unchanged natural-gas workpaper for separately admitted gas devices. Save the generator's consumed-fuel evidence and explicit default-heat-content eligibility through its new workpaper. The deterministic engine renders gas-level results with pinned candidate factors and rounding; the browser does not calculate emissions.
4. Demonstrate a missing workpaper, unsupported shared fuel relationship and an orphan corporate source. These prevent a positive equipment-reconciliation decision. The full source universe is the union of declaration rows, corporate stationary sources and saved gas/generator workpapers.
5. An eligible manager outside the cumulative contributor set reviews complete source workpapers and the exact equipment reconciliation separately. No self-approval or acceptance with unresolved blockers.
6. Correct a workpaper and show that prior reconciliation is stale until rebound and independently reviewed. Preserve exact older versions, decisions and reports. Label-only and explanation-only changes must follow the same correct successor rules as other meaningful changes.
7. Open and download the retained equipment report, source reports and data through the actual browser. Reports retain their original decisions and gaps after later corrections. Missing or corrupted lineage cannot pass merely because hashes agree.
8. Show readable desktop and narrow-screen forms, explicit progress/error messages and cleared data after actor/company changes. Verify late-response refusal, read-only/member behavior, signed-out access and server-enforced tenant boundaries.
9. Preserve existing schema18 records through the additive migration, then verify the actual hosted journey, real service restart, zero-write revisit and independent acceptance before publication completion.

## Scope and limits

- Accounting and technical contracts establish exact admissible gas devices, generator fuel evidence, primary-source locators, units, caps and calculations. Permissive old code is not evidence of domain approval.
- Reconciliation reports contain no aggregate emissions total. Gas and generator workpaper results remain bounded development candidates, with missing/unsupported facts visible.
- Refrigerants, fire suppression, process screening, corporate totals, source/method release and independent external assurance remain separate work. Neither this increment nor its synthetic fixture establishes a complete actual inventory or California legal compliance.
- No separate Nevada or Canada expansion. All sources inside the admitted corporate boundary remain relevant regardless of an interface's California focus.
- End with a working demonstration and board feedback before the next dependent milestone.
