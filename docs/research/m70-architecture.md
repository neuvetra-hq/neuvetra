Design candidate, 2026-09-15 UTC. Owner: M70-CTO, reporting to the root coordinator. Requested compute: registry critical `gpt-6-astra/high`; observed settings: unknown. This is a specification and gap assessment, not a deployed feature, approved accounting method, legal determination or assurance opinion. The coordinator's current board handoff supersedes the stale M68/M69 next-action fields in the operational notes.

## Outcome and scope

The corporate inventory becomes the organizing record: company group, reporting period, organizational boundary, entities, facilities, sources, all Scope 3 categories, requirements and evidence gaps. The first implementation increment must let an authorized preparer record that universe and an independent internal reviewer challenge an exact saved version. It does not yet calculate a complete corporate report.

M70 delivers this specification, its [machine-readable contract](m70-coverage-contract.json), product acceptance specification and a separate synthetic planning demonstration owned by the coordinator. No database, application route, factor or method is changed by these two artifacts. The final MVP remains the [board's complete acceptance target](../corporate-reporting-direction.md#first-actual-mvp-acceptance-target), including independent human assurance-provider validation of the handoff.

Initial proposed implementation profile, aligned with [CPO's brief](m70-product-brief.md): a U.S.-only, wholly owned office/distribution group with a California parent, one calendar reporting year, operational control and stable control during the year. The synthetic Juniper scenario uses 2025 and California/Nevada facilities. Record subsidiaries and operations in every geography; never restrict the register to California or omit a discovered foreign operation. Foreign activity, other consolidation approaches, ownership changes, unusual periods and unsupported activities are preserved as visible unsupported findings. The registration model supports effective dates from the start; automated allocation and calculations for complex boundary changes require later domain-reviewed methods. No real customer eligibility or SB 253 applicability is inferred from this profile.

Any future retention deletion is an explicit governed process, not cascading routine edits.

## Coverage semantics and lifecycle

An expected item is identified by tenant, inventory, boundary version, entity/source (or group-category screening), coverage domain and non-overlapping period segment. Screen Scope 1 stationary/mobile/process/fugitive classes, Scope 2 electricity/steam/heat/cooling with separate location/market method views, and Scope 3 categories 1–15. Screening rows establish the universe to investigate; they do not establish emissions or legal applicability. Category names follow the official [GHG Protocol guidance category index](https://ghgprotocol.org/scope-3-calculation-guidance-2), checked 2026-09-15; this taxonomy check does not approve any category method.

Keep these dimensions independent:

- **Disposition:** `unassessed`, `missing`, `included_activity`, `included_estimate`, `excluded`, `not_applicable`. A disposition is a preparer assertion. Excluded and not-applicable require reasons and supporting references, not a check box that silently removes a denominator.
- **Activity data:** `not_assessed`, `missing`, `partial`, `entered`, `estimate_proposed`, `explicit_zero`. Independent from disposition: an intended included activity can still have missing data; a proposed estimate can lack its inputs. `entered` is not validated completeness.
- **Evidence:** `not_assessed`, `missing`, `partial`, `linked`, `conflicting`. Linked means references resolve; sufficiency remains a separate reviewer judgment. A linked invoice can still conflict with input.
- **Method readiness:** `not_assessed`, `unsupported`, `candidate`, `released_for_use`. A candidate can be calculated in a synthetic demonstration while remaining unavailable for real reporting.
- **Review:** `unreviewed`, `changes_requested`, `accepted_bounded_internal`. Review applies to the exact version. Only an authorized distinct actor can accept it. New versions start unreviewed, including explanation-only changes.
- **Resolution:** `open`, `resolved_for_supported_profile`. Derived by deterministic validation from assertion, evidence, applicable reviewed criteria, method eligibility and findings. Unknown, unsupported or unsubstantiated items stay open. A gap can be clearly documented yet remain unresolved.
- **Corporate completeness:** `not_assessed`, `incomplete`, `ready_for_independent_assessment`. Derived globally, never client-editable. The foundation always returns `incomplete`; no percentage of completed rows can promote it.

`null` is unknown/missing; decimal `"0"` is an explicitly asserted zero activity and requires a period, unit and basis. Zero cannot substitute for a missing record. Estimates have their own assumption/rationale, input provenance, estimation method version, uncertainty description and reviewer finding; an estimate is not automatically excluded or deficient merely because it is estimated. Exclusions remain in the denominator and report with their reasons and assessed implications.

The CPO planning fixture deliberately uses presentation vocabulary, not the proposed API schema: `include` maps to `included_activity`, `estimate` to `included_estimate`, `exclude` to `excluded`, `unknown` to `unassessed`, and `pending` review to `unreviewed`. Its `data_state` maps to the separate activity-data dimension. A future adapter must also convert inclusive display end dates to the contract's exclusive end, validate complete references and reject ambiguous mappings. The fixture's string IDs and synthetic statements are not production evidence IDs. There is no implied direct import or persistence path from the demo.

Submission validates graph consistency and records the contributor identities for all content in the submitted version. Internal review rejects the submitter and any recorded contributor, not just the last person who clicked Save. A reviewer can request changes with open gaps, and may accept a bounded register while acknowledging its gaps; that acceptance never promotes completeness, method release or assurance. Final release/assurance policies are separate later gates.

## Applicability and requirements

Store customer facts separately from rules: organization type, fiscal/calendar year, corporate relationships, relevant jurisdictions/doing-business facts, revenue value/currency/period/entity aggregation basis when provided, and source evidence with assessed/unknown/conflicting states. Do not assume an SB 253 threshold, deadline, exemption, assurance level or effective date from historical notes.

Each requirement candidate must identify its authority (`statute`, `regulation`, `standard`, `agency_guidance`, `proposal`, `enforcement_statement`, `court_order`), source edition and locator, publication/effective/reporting dates, last-checked date and applicability reasoning. The statuses of legal character, source approval, interpretation review and product implementation are separate. Enforcement discretion is not stored as repeal. A missing source locator prevents an applicable requirement from being marked supported.

Maintain mapping: requirement version → required customer facts → affected coverage items → product behavior/component → acceptance case → observed validation evidence → remaining gap and owner. The foundation can display unresolved requirements and facts, but cannot say a customer is compliant. Qualified current regulatory/accounting review is a dependency before any real reporting profile is released.

## API and server contract

Proposed prefix: `/workspace/{companyId}/corporate-inventories`. This is a proposed interface; no route exists from M70. The JSON companion specifies representative payloads and normative invariants, not an executable JSON Schema or a deployed API.

1. `POST /` creates a synthetic draft from an explicit profile, period, boundary and register. Server supplies actor, UUIDs for version/audit, timestamp and derived findings. Entity/source stable UUIDs supplied for references are validated within the request; they never grant access.
2. `GET /{inventoryId}` returns current head and bounded version summaries. `GET /{inventoryId}/versions/{versionId}` returns the exact version and separately keyed review history. All references are authorized on each request.
3. `POST /{inventoryId}/versions` appends a complete replacement snapshot with expected head/version hash, correction reason and idempotency key. Omission of a previously registered entity/source requires a retained retirement/exclusion decision; it cannot silently delete coverage.
4. `POST /{inventoryId}/reviews` supplies target version/hash, decision, note and acknowledged finding IDs. Server captures the authorized reviewer. Acceptance requires the current head; later review of a historical version must be explicitly labeled historical and cannot approve a successor.
5. `GET /{inventoryId}/versions/{versionId}/coverage-export` returns a frozen coverage snapshot and manifest. Clearly identify `synthetic`, `incomplete`, `no_assurance`, and no emissions total. A later review creates another export snapshot if needed; old bytes remain unchanged.

Authorize bearer identity via the existing verified Auth boundary; ignore/reject actor or tenant identity in request bodies. Enforce current membership and staging admission again in the database transaction. Managers write; members read; outsiders receive a uniform 404 for inaccessible inventory resources. Unauthenticated requests return 401; authenticated insufficient operation permission 403; invalid payload 422; stale head/idempotency conflict 409; integrity/dependency failure 503 with no raw SQL/private details. Every response/download is private and no-store; unsafe origins are rejected. Search, jobs, caches and future source object downloads must share tenant enforcement; absent derived services are not presumed implemented.

Use the dedicated runtime role, forced RLS and narrowly granted security-definer mutations with fixed search paths. No direct table writes from browser roles. Inside the transaction, lock the tenant admission/membership rows in the same order as revocation, recheck access, then lock the inventory head; revocation and pending operations need a documented linearization point. Do not authorize a queued job forever from an old JWT. Browser state keys include authenticated subject, company and version; clear actor-owned state and discard late responses on identity/tenant change (L01).

## Canonical bytes, concurrency and immutable history

The new canonical profile is explicitly versioned; do not change historical M64–M68 hashes. Decode strict JSON with duplicate-key detection, reject unknown keys, non-finite numbers, unpaired Unicode surrogates and out-of-range values. Trim and normalize user-entered labels to NFC before validation; IDs are lowercase UUIDs, hashes lowercase hex. Dates are ISO date-only; instants canonical UTC millisecond strings. Amounts are finite decimal strings with per-field bounds; reject exponent notation and negative zero, remove insignificant fractional zeros for the new profile. Preserve the original submitted lexical value in separate evidence if needed. Existing fixed-scale arithmetic contracts remain unchanged.

Canonical JSON encodes object keys in deterministic lexical order, no insignificant whitespace, UTF-8 without BOM. Arrays declared sets are sorted by stable ID (category rows by category number, periods by start/end); duplicate IDs are errors. Ordered calculation steps retain their explicit sequence and cannot be reordered. Missing optional keys are normalized to explicit null only where declared; absence of a required key is an error. Domain validators run before hashing. These are a custom restricted profile, not a claim of RFC canonicalization compliance.

Use `content_sha256` for the effective user data after normalization; `version_sha256` for that content plus profile, predecessor, server version ID, creator and timestamp. Derived findings are reconstructed from pinned validator version and content and compared on read. Neither hash includes itself or later reviews. Reviews and exports have distinct hashes pinning the target version. Readback verifies the full graph, relational indexes, audit agreement and allowed lifecycle; changing bytes/hash/length together must still fail if provenance does not resolve (L02/L06).

Mutation fingerprint pins operation type, actor, tenant, inventory, expected head and effective payload. A tenant-scoped unique idempotency key records this fingerprint and outcome atomically. Same key/same operation returns the original outcome after current authorization, even if head later advances; same key/different fingerprint returns 409. For a new key, lock head, compare both expected version ID/hash, reject no-op based on the full effective input tuple, insert immutable version/audit/request and advance head in one transaction. Concurrent corrections have one winner; no partially written snapshots. Label-only, reason-only, source-only and estimation-basis-only changes are real successors. No recalculation or report changes happen in place.

## Calculation and final reported-total lineage contract

Each eventual reported line pins activity version and evidence/estimate basis, boundary and allocation version, service interval, original unit/value, conversion sequence/version, per-gas or already-CO2e semantics, factor ID/version/source-cell hash and applicability dimensions, method/version/implementation hash, GWP source/version/horizon where relevant, decimal policy, exact unrounded result and display rounding. No hidden latest factor, geographic substitute, control multiplier or double GWP conversion. Gas-mass inputs and already-converted CO2e are different typed quantities.

Future corporate totals have a `total_view` selecting Scope 2 location-based OR market-based; they never sum both. Carry Scope 1, each selected Scope 2 view and category-level Scope 3 separately with their own coverage. Offsets, avoided emissions and biogenic disclosures cannot silently net against gross totals. Their exact reporting treatment requires the released applicable method policy.

Detect duplicates at source hash, external account/document/line identifiers and activity identity/period overlap. A reused invoice can support multiple legitimately distinct activities, so a repeated evidence hash creates a relationship to review rather than globally forbidding reuse. Persist duplicate groups, suspected overlaps, reviewer disposition and allocation rationale. Leased assets, purchased energy and fuel-related activity can straddle scopes/categories; overlapping records must resolve using a pinned classification policy. Category inclusion never automatically resolves a competing Scope 1/2 entry.

Final pack graph: report bytes → report payload/renderer pin → inventory version/total view → reconciled coverage and requirements → included line/calculation versions → activity/allocation/unit/method/factor/GWP versions → exact original artifact locators or documented estimate basis. Include exclusions, non-applicability, open findings, corrections and review decisions. Manifest lists every file and byte hash with relative safe paths; replay reconstructs totals and claims from authoritative inputs. A matching archive hash alone is insufficient. This full pack is future work.

## Implementation sequencing and migration safety

1. **M70 review:** QA challenges these contracts and the coordinator's synthetic demonstration; board feedback defines the next bounded increment. No application/schema/source release occurs.
2. **Coverage foundation:** implement the corporate snapshot/typed indexes, entity/period/boundary/source register, complete category screening, independent statuses, current membership checks, immutable corrections/reviews and coverage-only export. Use synthetic fixture data and explicit feature gating. No arbitrary document uploads or new emissions totals.
3. **Evidence and requirements:** implement original artifact custody, real-document intake safety, fact evidence and qualified current requirements matrix; release sources separately. Freeze the initial customer profile only after representative customer discovery and domain review.
4. **Methods and reconciliation:** add independently reviewed Scope 1/2/3 methods in bounded increments; operational-control exceptions and unsupported categories stay visible. A company whose material required activity is unsupported cannot receive a complete report merely because the category row exists.
5. **Corporate report and assurance package:** validate representative supported companies, totals/replay, operational recovery, authorized external reviewer access and qualified human provider handoff. Only then evaluate the actual MVP target.

For the next migration, allocate the next number after checking the actual integration head (expected `0015`, not reserved by this document). Add tables and permissions; do not reclassify existing synthetic records or loosen their CA/2023 fixtures. Link a historical worksheet through an explicit legacy reference carrying synthetic/unreleased flags. Backup and restore the exact existing synthetic baseline; verify legacy record hashes/counts and access behavior after additive migration. Test migrations from schema 14 and clean setup with real PostgreSQL and the production driver. Expand readiness/manifest assertions together and test unknown/changed receipt refusal. Rollback disables new routes and leaves additive data preserved; no destructive down-migration after accepted writes. Real customer admission and multi-company rosters require separately reviewed operational/access changes.
