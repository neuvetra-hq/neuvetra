## Content and selection gates

1. **Reviewed display text.** Every displayed factual sentence comes byte-for-byte from the approved unit text or from existing reviewed evidence/qualification fields. No plan, verdict, explanation, question echo, raw provider error or unapproved prose becomes answer content. Do not silently shorten, rewrite or stitch clauses into new causal statements. Fixed connective/UI text must not add a factual relationship.
2. **Source sufficiency.** Independently review every unit's complete text against its own approved source closure, including modality, necessary prerequisites, causal or author-purpose attribution, absence scope and quantitative restrictions. Nearby units and generic disclaimers cannot cure an unsafe stand-alone claim. Required companion units may add necessary context but cannot contradict or retract the first unit's unqualified entitlement. If a unit requires a prerequisite in its own sentence, retain it there.
3. **Broad concepts.** Units describe reusable concepts and may support different paraphrases or combinations. No question strings, fixture IDs, hidden expected labels or keyword-specific acceptance paths enter the runtime catalog or selection prompt. A selection result is an untrusted proposed list of approved IDs, not a source approval.
4. **Deterministic closure.** Validate exact top-level and nested keys, known unique IDs, source/release/catalog identity, facet bindings, bounded size and complete acyclic companion closure before display. Reject unknown or malformed selections; do not silently drop invalid units or substitute nearest matches. A changed catalog or inactive/expired source cannot retain an earlier approval by filename alone.
5. **Whole-question coverage.** Selection must address all material requested facets, including a needed premise correction, without drowning an answer in unrelated units. A separate review may reject incomplete, irrelevant or out-of-scope composition but may not author a replacement. Correctly sourced irrelevant text is not a successful answer. A request mixing supported electricity material with an excluded method must not appear fully answered by the supported part.
6. **Truthful provenance.** The response/UI must distinguish a composition of reviewed explanations selected with AI from unrestricted AI-authored prose. Preserve exact unit/source references, edition, original-PDF locators, source and catalog hashes, cloud build identity and review limits. Source paragraphs remain evidence; an authored unit must not be presented as a quotation from EPA.
