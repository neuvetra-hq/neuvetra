## Bounded outcome

An authorized manager saves a fictional calendar-2025 California-only corporate reporting boundary with its expected entities, facilities, sources, screenings, synthetic evidence references and unresolved tasks; reloads it; makes traceable corrections; receives an exact-version internal review from an eligible different manager; and exports an unchanged historical coverage snapshot. The default example and board demonstration use only California entities and facilities. The foundation remains synthetic, incomplete, without emissions totals, released methods, legal applicability or assurance.

**Current board constraint, received during M71 planning:** finish the California workflow before Nevada/Canada support. Non-California discovery is solely a negative test: retain the discovered operation as an unsupported coverage gap rather than silently omitting it. It is not a featured expansion workflow or default scenario, and adds no regional method. This supersedes M70's broader proposed initial example; frozen M70 artifacts remain historical and unchanged. The eventual corporate boundary principle is preserved: unsupported operations cannot disappear from expected coverage.

**Latest clarification:** California-first applies to the reporting product, not a hard country/state filter on the company's inventory. The board permits recording non-California operations needed for that California reporting boundary. Keep the default demonstration California-only; retain necessary non-California entities/sources and identify unsupported methods without creating a separate Nevada/Canada regulatory product. The preceding negative-test restriction describes the default demonstration scope, not a prohibition on recording actual boundary discoveries. Current-law verification is owned by the regulatory reviewer/coordinator, not established by this acceptance document.

Basis: accepted [M70 product brief](m70-product-brief.md), [architecture](m70-architecture.md) and [coverage contract](m70-coverage-contract.json). M70 board acceptance and permission to continue are provided in the coordinator's current handoff; this document does not independently verify those conversation records. M71 implements the next production-code increment on existing infrastructure; it does not mean customer-data or accounting-method release.
