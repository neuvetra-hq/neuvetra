## Activation gates

Each case may become executable only after all of these gates pass for the exact case and reporting context:

1. A domain reviewer selects the applicable primary source version and pins the source table, row/cell locators, units, fuel identity, on-road/mobile boundary, heat-content basis where relevant, gas-specific factors and the program-specific GWP policy. Download/hash integrity alone is insufficient. CARB-MRR applicability and reporting-year use require separate regulatory review.
2. A reviewed extraction produces real factor records for the exact fuel, vehicle class, model-year band, geography, period and regulatory context. Factor IDs are assigned from those released records; the three current asserted IDs are unresolved strings and cannot serve as evidence. The factor release stays distinct from the raw source and extraction candidate.
3. The factor schema, processed-file contract and resolver add an allowed mobile factor type plus vehicle-class/model-year filters and canonical input-unit metadata. Migration, CSV and lookup behavior must agree and refuse absent or ambiguous matches.
4. The CNG pathway defines and tests an approved `scf` treatment. If it converts volume through heat content, the HHV/LHV basis and factor provenance must be explicit; a generic volume conversion is insufficient.
5. A deterministic mobile module is implemented with explicit precision and final-rounding policy, gas/CO2e reconciliation and factor/source provenance. Independent accounting derives each expected result from the approved source cells and checks substance, units, period, geography, boundary and GWP before the expectation enters the specification.
6. The strict harness then runs the activated cases, proves fail-closed behavior for wrong units/context and confirms that deferred cases remain visible but do not count as executable numerical coverage. Independent QA reviews the integrated bytes. Production or filing claims remain separate release gates.
