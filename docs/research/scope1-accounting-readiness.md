## Aggregation and evidence controls

The corporate numerator is gross Scope1 emissions from included sources; preserve gas-specific masses and compatible GWP conversion. Report biogenic CO2 separately when a later approved blend method supports it; do not remove associated CH4/N2O. Removals, offsets, avoided emissions and purchased certificates must not reduce this source sum. Scopes2/3 are separate; third-party transport and upstream fuel impacts are not smuggled into mobile factors.

Use a stable physical source identity across aliases and register versions. A source-period ledger selects exactly one effective calculation/estimate version; successor corrections replace, never add. A shared tank/meter cannot be assigned at full quantity to multiple sources, and annual evidence cannot be summed with the monthly records it summarizes. Cross-family quantities require explicit allocations whose parts reconcile to their evidence total. Reject incompatible year, inclusion interval, factor release or GWP policy from a common subtotal. Missing coverage may coexist with a clearly labeled partial subtotal, never a complete Scope1 claim.

The human handoff must include source census, control and period decisions, measured and estimated quantities, evidence identifiers/locators/hashes, reconciliation sheets, gas calculations, factor/GWP/method pins, precision/rounding, exceptions and uncertainty, correction history, exact reviewed versions and unresolved findings. Internal reviewer acceptance and mathematical reproducibility are not an assurance opinion. Root should preserve separate functional and release statuses even when every planned synthetic case passes.
