## CARB guidance: preserve the limits alongside the useful detail

The [September 1 guidance](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2026-09/2026_SB253_Reporting_Guidance.pdf) is first-cycle enforcement guidance. Page 1 conditions the initial regulation on OAL approval and separates discretion from statutory interpretation; page 2 describes November 10 with conditional wording. Treat that date as proposed pending verified approval/effectiveness, not a newly confirmed binding deadline.

Pages 1–3 describe voluntary intake/template use and alternate submittals, including existing reports/data and requested nonreporting statements. Page 4 retains statutory limited assurance while explaining first-year acceptance without assurance. Neither guidance nor a voluntary form repeals the statute.

Page 4 explicitly says EPA has not released eGRID2024 on its usual timeline and identifies official EPA eGRID2023, Cornerstone's separately published eGRID2024, or other credible factors as first-year options. Exact excerpt: “Reporting entities may use the most recent official U.S. EPA eGRID release (eGRID 2023) or, if preferable, the eGRID 2024 dataset”. The following text names Cornerstone and its use of EPA public source code. This is limited recognition for **2026 SB 253 reporting**, not general government approval or automatic acceptance for another framework/year.

All four pages were extracted and read. Pages 1 and 4 were rendered and visually checked; the coordinator separately read all four. The PDF's hyperlink annotations contain **no Cornerstone hyperlink**. Discovery continued through the named publisher's own announcement under the coordinator's explicit instruction.

The first-cycle public links now verified through CARB are the [report intake platform](https://survey123.arcgis.com/share/2a74000ea23b450ebf35c0f6f434445b?portalUrl=https://gis.carb.arb.ca.gov/portal), [draft template](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2025-10/SB253_Draft_Scope1_2_GHG_Template.xlsx) and [template memorandum](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2025-10/Draft%20Memo_Scope1%262TemplatePublicRelease.pdf). No report was submitted and the platform's transactional behavior was not tested. The retained workbook still calls itself a first draft and contains a historical October 2025 comment date; its fields are not automatically mandatory production requirements.

## EPA dataset and method implications

The [eGRID revision page](https://www.epa.gov/egrid/known-issues-data-notes-and-revisions) explains changes affecting biomass CO2e, negative generation, labels and plant crosswalks across revisions. It currently lists no known issues. That is a publisher statement, not proof that our parser or factors are correct. The retained [revision notes](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/other-files/2025-06/egrid2023_release_notes.txt) match inherited bytes but were absent from the 47-file bundle. Preserve revision 2 rather than selecting a workbook by year alone. The issues page and detailed-data page use differently spelled workbook links; use the successfully retrieved detailed-data URL and hash in the manifest rather than assuming every variant resolves.

The [v1.3 factor metadata](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/supply-chain-greenhouse-gas-emission-factors-v1-3-by-naics-6) identifies 1,016 commodities under 2017 NAICS, 2022 purchaser-price dollars, AR5 CO2e and separate with/without-margin fields. It excludes electricity, government and households. These release attributes must travel with any extracted factors. The [2020 EPA method report](https://cfpub.epa.gov/si/si_public_record_Report.cfm?Lab=CESER&dirEntryId=349324) was newly retained for background, with its publication date and version-specific limitations explicit; it is not newer than v1.3 documentation.

The [v2.5 model catalog](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/useeio-v2-5-models) describes import-coupled variants with different foreign models. Kinglet-22 uses EXIOBASE and detailed U.S. input-output data representing 2017, despite the model's 2022 target year. Its downloaded workbook has 33 worksheets including matrices, flows, indicators and crosswalks. The [model registry](https://github.com/USEPA/USEEIO/blob/master/models.md) distinguishes model variants, price basis and GWP indicators; generic version numbers cannot select the correct accounting dataset. Its table marks replacement waxwing v2.5.1 pending while a footnote describes it as replacing a deprecated release. Keep this publishing-status inconsistency visible and do not silently adopt that variant. Only the published Kinglet candidate was downloaded, not every model variant.
